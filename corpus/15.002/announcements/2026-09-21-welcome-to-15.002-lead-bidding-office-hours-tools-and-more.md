*Posted 2026-09-21 by Sheila Canalle*

***This announcement contains essential details about 15.002 — please read it carefully in its entirety.***

**MBA Class of 2028,**

Welcome to 15.002 Leadership Challenges for an Inclusive World (LEAD), a three-day required Core course designed to help you develop the skills, mindset, and self-awareness to lead with purpose in a complex, interconnected world. You'll learn from outstanding MIT Sloan faculty and carefully vetted external instructors who bring deep expertise, diverse perspectives, and real-world leadership experience to the classroom.

You'll also experience MIT firsthand by exploring labs and factories connected to the three MIT Presidential initiatives featured in this course and sharpen your professional development skills alongside the Career Development Office (CDO).

You will take 15.002 outside your usual cohort, giving you the opportunity to engage with peers from different backgrounds and dive into topics that challenge your knowledge, thoughts, and curiosity. LEAD may require some prep work prior to each session, but there is no homework or assignments afterward. As a result, active participation during the workshops is essential to getting the most out of this course.

**Attendance Policy: Attendance at all three days of LEAD is mandatory and required to pass this course. Attendance will be taken each day using A+ Attendance — please come prepared. For full policy details, refer to the LEAD Canvas site.**

**Course Overview**

- **Dates:** October 26–28 (Monday–Wednesday)

- **Time:** 9:00 AM–12:00 and 1:00-4:00 PM (lunch at noon, not included)

- **Structure:** Six workshops, two each day on:

-
  - Day 1: Leading Yourself and Leading with Others

-
  - Day 2: Leading with Impact: Presidential Initiatives and Professional Development

-
  - Day 3: Leading with Integrity

**Note**: All other Sloan Core courses will be paused this week.
