*Posted 2026-09-30 by Sheila Canalle*

Hi everyone,

Round 2 is now open. Here's what you need to know as you prepare for LEAD:

**Didn't bid in Round 1?** If you did not bid for LEAD, please use R2 to bid for your workshops. 15.002 is a degree requirement.

**Checking your LEAD bidding results:** Check successful bids at sloanbid.mit.edu (https://sloanbid.mit.edu) now that Round 2 is open.

**How your LEAD Schedule should look like**

- You should have 6 workshops in total (2 per day: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3).
  - **Day 1 and Day 3**: One Section A and one Section B (A = AM, B = PM).
  - **Day 2**: One CM workshop (no schedule) and one IM workshop. Your CM workshop will be allocated for the time you got your IM workshop — after bidding ends, the TA will allocate your CM workshop time to your Day 2 schedule.

- Students must build the correct schedule during LEAD bidding rounds.

**Missing workshops?**

- Use your remaining bid points in Round 2 to fill any gaps.
- Drop workshops that overlap so you can place bid points for others in R2 and fix your schedule.
- This round is not time-sensitive and closes **Friday, October 2 at noon**.
- The next bidding round will be add/drop, which is time-sensitive.

**Already have 6 with the right times and happy with your schedule?** You're all set — no further bidding needed!

**Resources**

- Review the LEAD Bidding Guidelines here (https://mysloan.mit.edu/v2/bidding/sip-lead-bidding-guidelines)
- Questions about LEAD bidding? Reach out to coursebid@mit.edu
