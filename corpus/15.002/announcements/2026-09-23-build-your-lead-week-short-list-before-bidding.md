*Posted 2026-09-23 by Sheila Canalle*

Hi everyone,

Round 1 bidding opens today at noon — you'll receive a separate message in your MIT Email when the portal is live. Here's how to set yourself up for a smooth process:

**Use the LEAD Workshop Planner (https://lead-workshop-planner-mba28.netlify.app/)**

Before you bid, build your short list using the LEAD Workshop Planner, linked in the left-hand menu on Canvas. The tool will show you exactly how each day will look based on your selections, so you can catch any conflicts before you're locked into bidding.

**Pay close attention to Day 2**

Day 2 has a specific structure — your short list for that day should include:

- One Presidential Initiative session
- One CDO session (paired with the plenary course for everyone)

Selecting anything outside this combination will throw off your Day 2 schedule, so double-check your choices in the Planner before entering your choices in the bidding portal.

**Day 1 and Day 3**

No restrictions — simply choose two workshops from the available list for each day.

**Round 1 timing**

Round 1 is not sensitive, so allocate all of your points during this round. Round 1 closes at noon on Monday, September 28 — make sure your full point allocation are in the portal before then.

Take a few minutes to build out your short list in the Planner first. It's the best way to confirm your picks will actually produce the schedule you want before bidding opens.

Good luck, and welcome to bidding with LEAD!
