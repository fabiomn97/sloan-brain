## Page 3

Project Finance Defined

- Project Finance is defined as:

“financing a single or a group of projects on a non-recourse basis through a

specially created single purpose entity”

- The project financiers are exposed to a single-purpose company.

- Recourse to the sponsor is generally limited

- Thus, the important governors to enable financing are:

- Contractual framework

- Technical reliability

- Consequently, a long-term financing contract is a primary prerequisite for successful project financing.

Technology Choice

- Technology choice is one of the most important considerations for financial institutions to get comfortable with project financing

- Solar PV v/s Solar Thermal
- Polycrystalline v/s Thin-film

- Newer technologies have a hard time to get financed because of lack of track record – old and boring is good!!

- Even with established technologies and manufacturers with strong track record, incremental innovations can be a lot of headache

- Newer businesses and start-ups have harder time to get their products financed due to weak balance sheet

- Risk Mitigation
- Bankability Analysis
- Fully wrapped turn-key EPC contracts
- Strong warranty package (serial defect clauses, power curve or heat rate guarantees, availability

guarantees, sound guarantees, etc.)
- Supplier credit to back guarantee claims
- Insurance package

## Page 15

Disadvantages of Project Finance Relative to Traditional Financings

- Longer execution timeframe

- High transaction costs

- Higher debt costs

- Intricate risk allocation resulting in complex documentation

- Significant lender oversight and delays in decision-making

- Stringent lender reporting requirements

- Higher insurance requirements and costs

- Limited manager discretion due to highly structured nature of the financing

- Greater disclosure of proprietary information and business strategies from the sponsor

## Page 19

Bond vs Bank Loan Financing in the Context of Renewables

- Bond financing is predicated on credit ratings
- Credit rating process is long and cumbersome. Certain bond rating firms such as KBRA or Fitch

can ease the pain
- Insurance companies are typical investors who are looking for longer dated exposure and are

generally content with NAIC ratings, which may reduce the execution timeframe and transaction costs

- Credit rating agencies have stringent underwriting criteria for renewable energy projects compared to banks

- Banks are often willing to finance renewable energy projects at 1.0x DSCR at P99 production level

or 1.30x DSCR at P50 production level (whichever that leads to lower up-front financing). Whereas, for investment grade rating credit rating agencies typically require 1.50x DSCR at P50 production level thereby constraining the up-front proceeds
- Bond financing can certainly reduce the financing costs due to broader marketability but the costs

are offset by longer and expensive financing process

- As a result, broadly speaking renewable energy projects are  primarily financed with bank loans rather than bonds

## Page 21

Moody’s Observations on Project Finance Default and Recovery Statistics

-  Moody’s notes that project finance loans have a relatively high default rate during the initial years (primarily due to inherent construction risks) and as projects gain a track record, the default risk goes down considerably – even lower than a typical A rated transaction

-  The historical distribution of project finance loan defaults illustrates that 2002 was a severe year for project finance loans (because many projects that had merchant exposure suffered defaults following the Enron crisis) but the recession beginning in 2008 only had a relatively modest impact.

-  Recovery rates in the event of default are estimated by Moody’s to be c. 80% overall (and c. 82-83% postconstruction).
