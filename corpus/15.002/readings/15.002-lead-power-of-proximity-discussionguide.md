15.002 LEAD- “Power of Proximity” Discussion Guide Objective of the course: To equip students to be leaders of transformative impact on organizations and the communities in which they operate. The course studies cases in which cross-sector partnerships of corporate, nonprofit and government leaders have attempted systems change to achieve robust social justice objectives – to varying outcomes. We examine the leadership strategies, approaches and proximity for achieving change. Content: Students will apply their knowledge of systems and their practice of leadership to examine examples of operational change that advances a social justice mission. Throughout the seminar, we study leadership exemplars in the for-profit, nonprofit and public sectors, and examine cases that invite an inquiry into complex social systems in areas from housing, to employment, to education, and criminal justice. We examine the systems design within and across these systems, the leadership models for partnership, and the outcomes for change. Framework for the Course: In a focus on Leadership Identity, we want to examine identity and interrogate who we hold up as leaders. The day will consist of three sections: Identify, intent & transferability, and integrity.

- Identity
- we want to begin by deconstructing identity - at the individual and organizational level
- Intent and Transferability
- how do we access the problem to be solved?
- how do we build the skills through collective wisdom for solving the challenge
- Integrity
- wrapping up with an analysis of systems, and an opportunity to guide individual decisions Pre-read Articles/Videos Below is a selection of 3 cases from the popular press, and one additional required pre-read. Each of these cases includes a social cause to be solved an by innovative business model. Please select two cases and preview the article/video for each. Consider the questions:

- What was the social cause/ problem to be solved?
- Who were the leaders for devising the solution?
- What was the investment or business model created to fund the solution?
- What were the targeted outcomes for the investment?
- What role did the leaders play in achieving outcomes?
