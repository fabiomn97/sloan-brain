EM.126 Pre-Reading

The debate resolutions, chosen by you, are listed below in no particular order. The links under each provide some suggested background reading.

1. Employers should accommodate their employees’ needs to an extent beyond what is in their own economic interest (e.g., by offering extended paid maternity/paternity leave or a “living wage”).

https://www.weforum.org/agenda/2024/05/why-companies-must-pay-living-wages/

https://www.cato.org/cato-handbook-policymakers/cato-handbook-policymakers-9th-edition- 2022/minimum-wage#non-wage-impacts-minimum-wage-hikes

2. The U.S. government should not provide tax credits for the purchase of electric vehicles with lithium batteries that require dangerous and environmentally harmful mining practices.

https://www.apmresearchlab.org/10x/lithium-mining-for-evs-sustainability

https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2024/10/electric-vehicle-subsidies-help-the-climate-u-sautomakers

3. Chief Financial Officers should not reduce their company’s tax burden by engaging in aggressive transactions that, although legal, are designed to obtain a tax result not intended by the government.

https://www.ibe.org.uk/resource/tax-avoidance-as-an-ethical-issue-for-business.html

https://www.nytimes.com/1970/09/13/archives/a-friedman-doctrine-the-social-responsibility-ofbusiness-is-to.html

4. Pharmaceutical companies should not profit from the sale of life-saving drugs.

https://www.brookings.edu/articles/addressing-the-trade-off-between-lower-drug-prices-andincentives-for-pharmaceutical-innovation/

https://www.npr.org/sections/goatsandsoda/2019/07/08/737786567/drug-prices-can-take-asurprising-turn-when-a-poor-country-gets-richer

5. Managers face no ethical obligation to obtain customers’ permission to use their data to improve their products or profitability.

https://www.brookings.edu/articles/companies-not-people-should-bear-the-burden-of-protectingdata/
