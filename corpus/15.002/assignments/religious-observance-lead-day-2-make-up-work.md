Lead Session: Data Analytics for good
Instructor Jeremy Ney

This assignment, developed by Jeremy Ney, challenges you to become 'Data Dangerous' by visualizing a critical social issue: US income inequality. Using the provided slides and the free tool Datawrapper, you will create a map of income inequality across US states or counties. The goal is to select your data, design a clear, compelling map, and use thoughtful color choices and classification to accurately and ethically communicate the severity and geographical distribution of wealth disparity.

**Submit the live link to your interactive Datawrapper (https://www.datawrapper.de/) map along with a brief written reflection (400-600 words) explaining your data source and key design choices**
