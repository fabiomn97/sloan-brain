##### **Assignment Description**

This make-up assignment is for students who were unable to attend the *Giving Voice to Values* (GVV) workshop on LEAD Day 3: Leading with Integrity.

It provides an opportunity to reflect on a real situation from a previous or current employer that involved ethical tension or values in action. Students will analyze their experiences using the *Giving Voice to Values* framework to explore how they acted—or could have acted—with integrity and effectiveness.
