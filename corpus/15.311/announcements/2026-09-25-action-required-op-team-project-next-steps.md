Good afternoon, Everyone,

I hope you enjoyed Team Day! Please see below for important next steps regarding the OP Team Project.

1. It is time to sign up for your team meetings with your OP Team Project Advisor.

Click here to access the sign-up sheet. (https://mitprod-my.sharepoint.com/:x:/g/personal/clide_mit_edu/IQC6t4If4hZMRIlh71V-z3mFAQGOBUW-x47WGLf07O_l-Ho)

All teams must sign up for TWO meetings with their assigned Project Advisor (one in October and one in November) **by 11:59 PM on Monday, September 28th.**

When signing up, please follow these instructions:

  -
    -
      - You can find your assigned Project Advisor on the first tab of the spreadsheet. For some teams, your Project Advisor differs from your classroom professor, so please check carefully.
      - One person from your team should go to the tab with your Project Advisor's name and enter the required information in your team's chosen time slots.
      - If there's a meeting time already claimed that is the only time your team is free, please reach out to that team to coordinate a swap. Do not edit, erase, or write over other teams' entries.

Once scheduling is complete, you will receive a calendar invite to confirm your meeting time. Each team will also receive a link to access a SharePoint folder. This is where you will post your deliverables two calendar days before each meeting with your advisor. Details on the deliverables can be found on Canvas (Home > OP Team Project > Major Deadlines - Fall 2026).

2. NDAs can trip up teams, so please review the guidance posted on Canvas. If your host requires an NDA, contact Ellen Baum (ebaum@mit.edu), who handles all MIT NDA signing. Do not go back and forth with your host company's legal team yourselves. Ellen will work on one NDA per team at a time.
3. Around October 1st, we will begin the process of identifying teams who need to work with one of the pre-sourced projects. If this applies to you, please note that you must still sign up for both meetings with your Project Advisor before the deadline (we recommend selecting a later October time slot to make your first meeting as productive as possible).

**Questions?**

Please reach out to your Teams TA or your Project Advisor, and include your Cohort and Team name in the subject line so we can better assist you.

Thank you.
