I hope your projects are going well and that you’re making good progress with your host company leads. If you’re having trouble securing a host, we have a few pre-sourced projects available.

Submit your request for a pre-sourced project by **Monday, 10/05 at 12:00PM ET** using this form (https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdD7qU__2SXSkT4RHehJ-KBAO1v97cGnkUQnYctTEQCIQLiKQ/viewform?usp=header)
