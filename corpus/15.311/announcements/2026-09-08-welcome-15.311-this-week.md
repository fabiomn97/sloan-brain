*Posted 2026-09-08 by Chelsea Lide*

Hello Mediterranean & Pacific Cohorts,

Welcome to MIT Sloan! I hope you had a great orientation and look forward to meeting each of you later this week. Please see below for some important information about our upcoming sessions.

We have 15.311 Organizational Processes (OP) on Thursday *and* Friday this week. Note that both Thursday and Friday are at special times and/or locations (refer to the syllabus for more details).

**Thursday, September 10th**

- **What to expect:**
  - We will start by discussing the Lululemon case to introduce the "Three Lenses" framework that provides the foundation for our course. In the second half of our double-session, I will provide an overview of OP, review some class logistics, and introduce a major component of this course: the OP Team Project.

- **What to prepare:**
  - Read the Lululemon case and the article "The Three Lenses: A Guide to Understanding Organizations." Come to class ready to discuss the following:
    1. What is going on in the market? What are the company's goals?
    2. Using the three lenses framework, how would you diagnose the structural design, culture, and political system under Wilson versus under Meers?
    3. What actions would you take if you were coming in as Day? Why?

**Friday, September 11th**

- **What to expect:**
  - We will focus on networks and how to build and leverage them, both as leaders and in managing your own careers.

- **What to prepare:**
  - Read the sections of the article "A Political Perspective on Organizations" that are related to networks. There is no other preparation required for Friday.

Again, I want to extend you a warm welcome to Sloan -- I look forward to a fabulous semester together!

See you on Thursday,
Professor Lide
