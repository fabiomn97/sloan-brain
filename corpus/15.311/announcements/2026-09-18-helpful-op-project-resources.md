Hi Meds!

Hope the semester is off to a great start and you've gained some traction sourcing companies for the Organizational Processes Project! I'll be your TA specifically for the OP Project this semester.

To support your sourcing efforts, we've compiled a couple of resources:

**Project Information PDF:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oFvKoWjRHRcY3VbMNkOTEccEF9lWs9Qk/view?usp=drive_link
This document outlines what makes a strong host company and project for OP. We'd encourage you to review it as a team. It should help clarify the kind of companies and projects that fit well, and make your conversations with potential hosts more focused. Feel free to share this PDF with companies as a reference when reaching out.

**Sample Projects Sheet:** https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1H_NcagkPS5LWEF4ltVftBV0pv2TpQJg9/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=104320082232214183095&rtpof=true&sd=true

This spreadsheet lists sample projects from past OP cohorts. It's meant purely as a representative guide to the kind of initiatives that make for a strong, relevant project, and not an exhaustive or prescriptive list.

Good luck with your search! As always, reach out if you have questions about contacting companies, scoping a project, or anything else along the way. If you have timeline or other general questions about the project, use the OP Project Bot in Canvas.
