WAKEFIELD MEDICAL

Team Decision Exercise

ABSTRACT Wakefield medical needs to hire a new Senior Vice President of Finance. Which candidate would be best for this position?

## Page 2

GENERAL INFORMATION Wakefield Medical manufactures mobile clinical workstations used in hospitals. Nurses are the primary users of Wakefield carts, moving them between patient rooms, nursing stations, and the hospital pharmacy.  Each mobile cart contains a mounted laptop for entry and display of critical patient data as well as an auto-locking drawer system that integrates with hospital pharmacy systems for controlled medication dispensing. Wakefield carts are known for their sophisticated ergonomic designs, advanced battery technology that enables power-assisted mobility, and advanced anti-microbial material on all exposed surfaces. With hospitals’ increasing adoption of electronic medical records, demand for mobile medical carts has grown considerably in recent years, and Wakefield has experienced 20% growth in revenues and profits over the last 3 years. Also, Wakefield recently acquired one of its largest competitors. This growth has created challenges for the company’s infrastructure, but, for the most part, Wakefield has managed its growth well, and industry analysts consider it the industry leader. A few weeks ago, Wakefield’s Senior Vice President of Finance stepped down unexpectedly for personal reasons. The SVP of Finance reports to Wakefield’s CEO and is responsible for all of Wakefield’s financial reporting, cost control, and strategic finance needs. The position is one of 6 SVP positions that report to the CEO:

CEO

SVP Sales SVP Marketing

SVP Operations SVP R&D SVP Finance

SVP Business Development

VP - Cost Accounting

VP - Financial Reporting

You are one of the other 5 SVPs at Wakefield. The CEO has asked all 5 of you to work together to pick a new SVP of Finance. The new SVP of Finance must continue to build an industry-leading finance organization, and will need to balance the sometimes conflicting demands from Wakefield’s other divisions as the company continues to grow. You’re about to begin your meeting with the other SVPs. When it concludes, your group will have to present just 1 recommendation to the CEO. Your group has narrowed the list to three candidates. To prepare for the meeting, review the information about the three candidates a) from the general information provided below, and b) from the specific information you possess in your role.
