Come to class prepared to discuss the following questions:

1.
  1. What does it take to be successful in the mobile data network service industry and what are the key success factors for Connective Mobility?
  2. Do you agree that Connective Mobility needed more structure when Brew joined? Why or why not? Was a holacracy a first good move?
  3. For the functional and divisional structures presented in the case, how are tasks grouped? How are the organizational units linked/integrated with one another? What are the strengths and weaknesses of each structural form?
  4. Do you think Brew should be fired? Why or why not? What would you have done similarly and differently from Brew? Do you agree with his structure philosophy?
  5. Real world experiences: Have you undergone organizational restructuring? What went well, and what would you have done differently?
