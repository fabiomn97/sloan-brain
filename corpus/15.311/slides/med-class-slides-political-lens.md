## Page 7

Political Toolkit: Positional Power

-  Power that comes from your formal place in the hierarchy

Sources of Power

Influence Tactics

- Decision rights

- Shape behavior with rewards and punishments

- Resource allocation

- Use position to remove critics

- Information flow

- Task assignment

- Assign integrators to bridge between different interest groups

- Performance evaluations

But, beware of the paradox of positional power!

## Page 8

Political Toolkit: Personal Power

-  Power that comes from your skills and abilities

Sources of Power

Influence Tactics

- Subject-matter expertise

- Use facts and evidence to persuade

- Positive track record

- Social skills or “charisma”

- Appeal to others’ principles, ideals, and aspirations

- Show deference to make the other person feel important

## Page 9

Political Toolkit: Network Power

-  Power that comes from your connections and access to others

Sources of Power

Influence Tactics

- Network breadth

- Send emissaries to smooth the way

- Network depth

- Structural power

- Leverage opinion leaders to provide social proof

- Knowledge of the landscape

- Make exchanges
