How should you restructure?

-  Determine your strategic goal (e.g., efficiency? coordination?)

- Groups: project teams, task forces

- Roles: liaisons, project managers

- Technologies: collaboration and communication tools

## Page 22

How should you restructure?

-  Determine your strategic goal (e.g., efficiency? coordination?)

-  Consider linking mechanisms as potential alternatives to formal restructuring

-  Use all three lenses to promote more effective change management

Ø Structural Design: Clarify objectives and process of structural change

Ø Politics: Secure support from key stakeholders to minimize opposition

Ø Culture: Articulate vision that links new structure to existing values

## Page 23

How should you restructure?

-  Communication is key

- Understanding your audience facilitates empathy (e.g., create stakeholder

“personas” to anticipate reactions, resistance, needs, etc.)

- Give people multiple opportunities to learn about + process the change

- Prep leaders at all levels to deliver consistent, accurate information

- Be explicit about the problem, and why restructuring is the best solution

-  Beware: leaders often have a bias toward under-communication

(This can make you seem less caring + competent in the eyes of your employees)
