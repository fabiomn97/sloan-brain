## Page 8

Takeaways: Bottom-Up Change

-  Structural Design Lens - Establish who is in the organization and how they are connected - “Trickle down” influence on subordinates - Communication is critical – Briefings, mass mailing + advice, floor visits, workshops

-  Political Lens - Understand the concept of critical mass - Identify early adopters, champions, gatekeepers, resisters - Build relationships with local opinion leaders, and use local networks to persuade key stakeholders

-  Cultural Lens - Norms matter, even if they seem like a waste of time (like importance of face-to-face meetings) - Because Mary Bolt wants to “increase innovation within the company and to shift the culture in the direction of distributed leadership,” CEO decree never helps + often hurts.

Structure

The team’s processes, systems, and allocations of roles/responsibilities to get the work done.

- Have you already picked roles needed to manage the work across the core?

The team’s culture, the camaraderie between team members, and their ability to consistently collaborate.

- What norms do you want to follow?

- Can you raise concerns early, before they fester?

- How will you give feedback?
