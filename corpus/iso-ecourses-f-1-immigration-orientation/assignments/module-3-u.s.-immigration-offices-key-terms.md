**U.S. Immigration Offices & Key Terms**

- **U.S. Department of Homeland Security**: DHS (https://www.dhs.gov/) is the “Parent” office of ICE, CBP, and USCIS.
- **U.S. Immigration & Customs Enforcement**: ICE (https://www.ice.gov/) enforces federal laws governing border control, customs, trade, and immigration.
- **U.S. Customs & Border Protection**: CBP (https://www.cbp.gov/) oversees immigration customs at U.S. borders (land, sea, and air).
- **U.S. Citizenship & Immigration Services**: USCIS (https://www.uscis.gov/) administers naturalization and immigration authorization systems (e.g., AT, H-1B, etc.).
- **U.S. Department of State**: DOS (https://www.state.gov/) is responsible for the country's foreign policy and relations. Oversees U.S. Embassy and Consulate offices worldwide.
- **U.S. Department of Labor**: DOL (https://www.dol.gov/) is responsible for administering federal laws governing occupational safety and health, as well as wage and hour standards.
- **Student & Exchange Visitor Program**: SEVP (https://www.ice.gov/sevis) manages the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System.
- **S****tudent & Exchange Visitor Information System**: SEVIS (https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub) is the web-based system for maintaining information on international non-immigrant students and exchange visitors to the U.S.
