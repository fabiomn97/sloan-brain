**Module 7: Off-Campus Work/Employment For F-1 Students**

**REQUIRED: A student must confirm that they *"have read and understand all the information within this module”* by marking this module as "DONE" at the bottom of the page.**

|   |

**"Off-Campus" Work/Employment Authorization Restrictions**

U.S. federal regulations place ***strict limitations*** on work/employment opportunities for international students in the U.S. ***All work/employment MUST be authorized in advance, including but not limited to***:

- All paid or unpaid opportunities.
- All on-campus or off-campus opportunities.
- All work/employment for organizations inside or outside the U.S.
- **Prior authorization *MUST* be obtained *BEFORE* engaging in any "Off-Campus" work/employment-related activities**.

**IMPORTANT**: Engaging in work/employment without prior authorization is a ***serious violation*** of a student’s immigration status. ***Consequences include, but are not limited to***:

- Termination of a student’s U.S. immigration status.
- Students’ inability to continue or complete their MIT program.
- Loss of future U.S. immigration benefits (e.g., CPT, OPT, H-1B, etc.).

*****Remember, always consult with an*** ***ISO Advisor*** (https://iso.mit.edu/about-iso/student-advisors-by-program/) ***BEFORE accepting or beginning any work/employment opportunities while in F-1 status*****

|   |

**"Off-Campus" Work/Employment Eligibility & Requirements**

***What Is Considered “Off-Campus” Work/Employment?***

- "Off-Campus" work/employment refers to ***ANY*** work/employment activity that ***DOES NOT*** meet the criteria for "On-Campus" work/employment (as outlined in Module 6). This includes internships, jobs, or research positions located outside of MIT or not directly paid by MIT.

***Advanced Planning is Essential***

- Authorization for "Off-Campus" work/employment requires advance approval. **Students are strongly advised to begin planning at least *three to four months* *BEFORE* their intended start date**.
- For eligibility details, application steps, and timelines, visit the ISO website (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/).

|   |

**“Off-Campus” Work/Employment Eligibility**

All new/incoming international students (https://iso.mit.edu/getting-started/orientation-program/#WhoNewStu) become eligible for "Off-Campus" work/employment, also referred to as “Practical Training,” if they meet all of the following criteria:

- Are enrolled in an MIT degree program.
- Have maintained valid F-1 status within the U.S. for one full academic year (i.e., two academic terms such as fall and spring).
- Have maintained good academic standing in their MIT program.
- Have complied with all F-1 immigration regulations, including ensuring that any "Off-Campus" work/employment is ***directly related (https://iso.mit.edu/knowledge-base/for-f-1-off-campus-employment-all-positions-be-directly-related-to-my-mit-degree-major-field-of-study-is-there-any-guidance-on-how-to-determine-directly-related/)*** to their major field of study.

***“Off-Campus” Work/Employment Restrictions***

- **New/Incoming Students**
  - All new/incoming international students are ***NOT*** eligible to apply for any “Off-Campus” work/employment (whether paid or unpaid) until completing two full academic terms (e.g., fall and spring) in F-1 status at MIT and while physically present in the U.S.
    - **NOTE****:** In addition to the two-term requirement, undergraduate students ***MUST*** officially declare a major, approved by both the academic department and the Registrar, ***BEFORE*** becoming eligible to apply for “Off-Campus” work/employment authorization.

  - **ATTENTION**: **International students who begin their MIT program in Fall 2026 will become eligible to apply for “Off-Campus” work/employment authorization *no earlier than Summer 2027***.

|   |

**Types of Off-Campus Work/Employment**

***What Options Are Available?***

International students may be eligible for the following types of "Off-Campus" work/employment, based on their academic and immigration status:

- **Curricular Practical Training (CPT (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/f-1-curricular-practical-training/))**
  - ***MUST*** occur ***during*** the degree program.
  - ***MUST*** be an ***integral part*** of the curriculum and earn academic credit toward degree requirements.

- **Optional Practical Training (OPT)**
  - May be used during or after the degree program.
  - Includes three (3) distinct types:
    - **1) Pre-Completion OPT (Pre-OPT (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/f-1-optional-practical-training-during-program-pre-completion-opt/))**: Only available ***during*** the degree program. **NOTE**: It can ***ONLY*** occur within the program start and end dates listed on page 1 of the MIT Form I-20.
    - **2) Post-Completion OPT (Post-OPT (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/f-1-optional-practical-training-after-program-post-completion-opt/))**: Only available ***after*** completion of the degree program. **NOTE**: It can ***ONLY*** begin after the program end date on page 1 of the MIT Form I-20.
    - **3) STEM OPT Extension (STEM OPT (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/f-1-stem-optional-practical-training-stem-opt-extension/))**: Only available ***after*** an active period of Post-Completion OPT. **NOTE**: It is ***ONLY*** for students with eligible STEM degrees (https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/stem-opt-hub/additional-resources/eligible-cip-codes-for-the-stem-opt-extension).

For detailed eligibility requirements and application guidance, refer to the ISO website (https://iso.mit.edu/employment/).
