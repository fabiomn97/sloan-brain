**Module 1: F-1 Immigration Orientation eCourse Overview**

**REQUIRED: A student must confirm that they *"have read and understand all the information within this module”* by marking this module as "DONE" at the bottom of the page.**

|   |

**Online Immigration Orientation & Check-In Requirements**

To ensure accessibility and compliance with U.S. immigration regulations, the International Students Office (ISO) provides all ***required*** immigration orientation (https://iso.mit.edu/getting-started/orientation-program/) materials for new/incoming F-1 and J-1 degree-seeking students via the Canvas platform (https://canvas.mit.edu/) and the iMIT Portal (https://imit.mit.edu/).

***Who Needs to Complete the Orientation and Check-In***

The following students are considered *New/Incoming International Students* (https://iso.mit.edu/getting-started/orientation-program/#WhoNewStu:~:text=Additional%20Information-,WHO%20IS%20CONSIDERED%20A%20%E2%80%9CNEW/INCOMING%20INTERNATIONAL%20STUDENT%E2%80%9D%3F,-New/Incoming%E2%80%9D%20students), and they must complete an online immigration orientation and submit the required documentation to the ISO before starting their program at MIT:

- Students entering the U.S. in F-1 or J-1 status to begin a program at MIT.
- Students returning from a leave of absence (RLOA) in F-1 or J-1 status.
- Students transferring their SEVIS record (F-1 or J-1) to MIT from another U.S. institution/school**.**

|   |

**F-1 Immigration Orientation eCourse (Canvas)**

This online course is designed exclusively for F-1 students. If you are ***NOT*** an MIT degree-seeking student in F-1 status, you should ***NOT*** complete this eCourse.

***Purpose of the eCourse***

- To fulfill the U.S. regulatory requirement that all new F-1 students complete an immigration orientation.
- To provide a comprehensive overview of the rules, responsibilities, and best practices for maintaining lawful F-1 status in the U.S.
- Students must achieve a score of 90% or higher on the associated quiz to complete this course.

***eCourse Quiz Requirements***

- A passing grade of ***90% or higher is required***.
- The quiz consists of 20 “True” or “False” questions.
- Students are allowed ***up to five (5) attempts*** to pass the quiz.
  - Students who do not pass the quiz after five attempts must contact the ISO at iso-help@mit.edu, as they will then be ***required*** to retake the eCourse.

***Saving The Quiz Results***

- Students are responsible for saving proof of their passing quiz results. ***DON'T FORGET!***
- **The quiz result *MUST* clearly show**:
  - The student’s ***full name***.
  - The ***date***.
  - The ***quiz score***.

- **ATTENTION**: Saved quiz results ***MUST*** match the example image shown below.
  - Students can view their grade results by selecting “***Grades***” from the page sidebar and then clicking the “***Print Grades***” button.
  - Acceptable formats include a screenshot or saving the grade results as a PDF.

**Immigration Online Check-In (iMIT)**

Once in the U.S., F-1 students must complete the *Immigration Online Check-In (https://iso.mit.edu/getting-started/#:~:text=Required%20Online%20Check%2DIn)* by submitting all required documentation through their iMIT Portal (https://imit.mit.edu/) account.

***Important Online Check-In Guidelines***

- This *Immigration Online Check-In* can ***ONLY*** be submitted ***AFTER*** a student enters the U.S. in F-1 status and has completed the *F-1 Immigration Orientation eCourse and Quiz*.
- **IMPORTANT****:** Students can ***ONLY*** enter the U.S. within **30 DAYS** of the program start date listed on their MIT Form I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility) document.

***How to Complete the Immigration Online Check-In***

To fulfill the *Immigration Online Check-In* requirement, students ***MUST*** follow the steps below:

- Log in to their iMIT Portal (https://imit.mit.edu/) account via "*Full Client Services for Students*."
- Select the "*Upon Arrival*" tab on the left-hand sidebar.
- Select the ***correct*** *Immigration Online Check-In* e-Form.

- Students ***MUST*** select the e-Form that matches their program start term and year.
  - **Example**: If a student’s program starts in Fall 2025, they can ***ONLY*** submit the "*Fall 2025 Check-In*" e-Form.
    - If a student’s program starts in Fall 2025 and only the "*Summer 2025 Check-In*" e-Form is listed, the student ***MUST*** wait until they are within ***30 days*** of the program start date listed on their Form I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility) ***BEFORE***

  - Select "*Start a New Request*" and complete all three (3) sections and e-Forms listed below:
    - **Section 1: Student Status**
      - Complete the "*Student Status*” e-Form

    - **Section 2: Student Category**
      - Complete the "*New/Transfer Students*" e-Form

    - **Section 3: Online Check-In Forms**
      - Complete the "*1. Your Information*" e-Form
      - Complete the "*2. Passport Information*" e-Form
      - Complete the "*3. Visa Stamp Information and Upload*" e-Form
      - Complete the "*4. Local U.S. Address Information*” e-Form
      - Complete the "*5. Upload Your I-94 Electronic Record/Card (After Arrival)*" e-Form
      - Complete the "*6. Upload Your I-20 or DS-2019 or Visa Category*" e-Form
      - Complete the "*7. Your Immigration Status*" e-Form
