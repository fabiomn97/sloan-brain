**Welcome!**

A warm welcome to all new members of the MIT international community!

|   |

**About the International Students Office (ISO)**

The ISO provides guidance and support to help international students maintain their U.S. immigration status while studying at MIT.

- As part of the MIT community (not the U.S. government), the ISO’s goal is to help students understand and meet federal requirements without adding unnecessary barriers.
- The ISO is committed to advocating for international students and supporting a smooth transition to life at MIT and in the U.S.

|   |

**Beyond Immigration**

The ISO recognizes that each student brings unique experiences and aspirations beyond their immigration status.

- Through various programs and events (https://iso.mit.edu/news-and-announcements/events/), the ISO fosters connections and engagement across the MIT community.
- Weekly e-newsletters (https://iso.mit.edu/about-iso/newsletters/) keep international students informed about important immigration updates, campus events, and ISO programs.

***Wishing all new international students a wonderful and inspiring start to their academic journey at MIT.***
