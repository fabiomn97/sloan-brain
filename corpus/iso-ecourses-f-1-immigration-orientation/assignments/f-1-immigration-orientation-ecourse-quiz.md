**Points:** 100.0

**QUIZ REQUIREMENTS**

- A passing grade of ***90% or higher is required***.
- The quiz consists of 20 “True” or “False” questions.
- Students are allowed ***up to five (5) attempts*** to pass the quiz.
  - Students who do not pass the quiz after five attempts must contact the ISO at iso-help@mit.edu, as they will then be ***required*** to retake the eCourse.

**SAVING THE QUIZ RESULTS**

- **The quiz result *MUST* clearly display the student’s Full Name, Date, and Quiz Score. Review the image example in Module 12.**
  - Students are responsible for saving proof of their passing quiz results in PDF format. ***DON'T FORGET!***
  - Students can obtain their final grade results by selecting “***Grades***” from the page sidebar and then clicking the “***Print Grades***” button.
