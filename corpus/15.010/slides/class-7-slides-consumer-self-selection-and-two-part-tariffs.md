## Page 32

Reminders for the Lab

- Profit is maximized where marginal revenue = marginal cost

- For a linear demand curve p = a − bq, marginal revenue is MR = a − 2bq: same intercept, twice the slope

- Potential consumer surplus is the triangle under demand

- A customer joins if potential consumer surplus ≥ F  (if it equals F, assume they join)

- Total profit = per-customer profit × number of customers: (p − MC) × units × customers, plus F × customers who join

When Does a Large Fee Work?

One type of customer (e.g., all heavy users)

Set p = MC so they enjoy the product as much as possible Charge a large fee that takes their whole surplus

Different types (light and heavy users)

Keep the fee low, or zero, so everyone joins Charge a price above MC to take surplus from the heavy users

Printers and Ink: Heterogeneous Users

The printer is the entry fee F; ink is the per-unit price p

Users differ: some print a few pages a month, an office prints all day

A cheap printer gets everyone in; ink priced well above its cost earns most from the heavy users

An expensive printer and cheap ink would lose the light users and give up the margin on the heavy ones
