## Page 81

Vocabulary: Deadweight Loss

Deadweight loss: lost surplus due to market distortion (too many or too few trades)

Distortions include: market power, price controls, taxation, information problems, and externalities

We will cover several of these in H2, along with the public policies that address them (taxes, antitrust, regulation)
