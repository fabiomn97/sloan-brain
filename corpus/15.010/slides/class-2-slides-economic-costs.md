## Page 25

Economies of Scale vs Scope

Scale

Scope

Unit cost falls as you produce more of the same good. Example: a car plant’s assembly line and tooling spread over more cars.

Shared inputs lower the cost of producing different goods. Example: one platform, engine and dealer network shared by a carmaker’s sedans and SUVs.

Economies of scale and scope give firms competitive advantage

Q3: Is the GPU a Fixed or a Variable Cost After All?

No rental market

A rental market at $1.25B a month

$15.8B of GPU purchases become depreciation, $1.9B a quarter, whatever the number of tokens served.

Fixed
