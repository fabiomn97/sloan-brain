## Page 67

Elasticity Estimates

Own-price Cross-price Elasticity Elasticity

A 1% increase in Coca- Cola’s price lowers the quantity of Coke −1.47 +0.52 demanded by 1.47%

A 1% increase in Pepsi’s price raises the quantity of Coke −1.51 +0.65 demanded by 0.52%

Source: Gasmi, Laffont and Vuong (1992)
