MARKET SEGMENTATION

Market segmentation is just one instance of price discrimination. By charging different prices to segments of the market with different elasticities, you can increase profit. In the lab, demand in the EU is more elastic than in the US, so the optimal price is lower there, and charging each market its own price earns more than one price for both. The price is higher for the segment with less elastic demand; the level of demand does not matter for optimal pricing, only elasticity and marginal cost do. Examples are student, senior and resident discounts.
