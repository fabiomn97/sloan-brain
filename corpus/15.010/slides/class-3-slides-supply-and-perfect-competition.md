What if supply and demand given: Algebra

We can also find equilibrium P and Q using algebra

Suppose we have demand and supply equations:

Quantity demanded: Qd = 100 − 2P

Quantity supplied: Qs = 10 + P

How to find the equilibrium price and quantity?

15.010 ·Class 3 ·45

## Page 46

Algebra Example Solution

1 At equilibrium, Quantity Demanded = Quantity Supplied Q*d = Q*s

100 − 2P = 10 + P 3P = 90 Equilibrium Price (P*) = 30

2 Substitute P* into (either) Qd or Qs to find eq. quantity Q*

From Demand Curve: Q* = 100 − 2P* Equilibrium Q* = 40

From Supply Curve: Q* = 10 + P* Equilibrium Q* = 40
