## Page 18

Key Takeaways:

- Menus allow to offer plans that appeal to different types

- Caps reduce the appeal of low-end plans to high-wtp buyers, enabling firms to charge them more for the unlimited plan

- Creating vs. capturing value: it can be worthwhile to create some inefficiency in plans aimed at low-wtp buyers, so to capture more value from high-wtp buyers
