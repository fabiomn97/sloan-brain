**Project #1: Electricity Market Supply, Demand, and Entry**

You are considering building a data center in New England. This project works up to that decision using real-world data: you build the region's electricity supply curve, see what sets the price in a given hour, and then ask what a facility of this size would pay for power and whether the project is worth building.

All seven questions, the data dictionary, and Figures 1-2 and Table 1 are in the **Project 1 Questions** PDF, posted with the two data files in this module.

|  **Due** |  **5:00 pm on Wednesday, September 23.** |

|  **Submission** |  One team member submits on Canvas. Name both files after your core team. |

|  **In class** |  Class 5. Each team presents one question. Be prepared to present your results. |

**Data files**

- **operating_plants_marginal_cost.csv**: marginal cost, capacity, and fuel type for all 1,164 New England plants operating in December 2025.
- **planned_plants_capacity.csv**: the 336 New England plants under construction as of August 2026.
