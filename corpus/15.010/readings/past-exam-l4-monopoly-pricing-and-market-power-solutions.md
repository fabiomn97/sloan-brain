2. 2023_midterm: Pricing in two countries

You're a monopolist looking to launch your product in two new countries: Country A and Country

B. Your market research team tells you that the price elasticity of demand is constant, at -1.3 in Country A and -1.5 in Country B. Consumers cannot resell the product across the border. You have a constant marginal cost for production in both countries, but you don't know what that cost is.

a) Would you set different prices in each country? If so, which country would have the higher

price? Why?


Since the demand in Country B is more elastic (in absolute terms, -1.5 is greater than -1.3), consumers there are more sensitive to price changes. Therefore, to maximize profits, you would set a lower price in Country B than in Country A.

No, in order to find the optimal price, we need the marginal cost of production. Once we

know the marginal cost, we can use the markup formula to set a profit maximizing price.

b) A monopolist is deciding on what price to charge for its product. Briefly explain why marginal revenue differs from price.


Marginal revenue is the change in revenue divided by the change in output. To increase output, the monopolist must lower the price, so there is a gain in revenue (due to the

additional Q) but a loss in revenue due to the change in P.
