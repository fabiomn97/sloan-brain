We will take the example we saw in class. There’s two types of consumers, those with high

willingness to pay (type H) and those with low willingness to pay (type L). The inverse demand for

type H is given by p = 1 −0.01QH (so their demand is QH = 100 −100p) and the inverse demand

for type L is given by p = 0.5 −0.01QL (so their demand is given by QL = 50 −100p). Marginal

cost, denoted by c, is $0.10 per unit (i.e. per cell phone minute). There are 900 consumers with low

willingness to pay (NL = 900, where NL is the number of consumers with low willingness to pay),

and 200 consumers with high willingness to pay (NH = 200, where NH is the number of consumers

with high willingness to pay).
