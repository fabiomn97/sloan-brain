### Reflection Worksheet for Leadership Foundations

The worksheet helps you distill learning from the x360 and reflect on your values, aspirations, and lived experience, to guide you in shaping a deliberate leadership path at Sloan.

Upon completion, please submit this worksheet in Canvas.

You'll be then assigned a guide (faculty, staff, or alum) for a 90-minute discussion to explore your strengths and challenges without critique. We hope you get to enhance your leadership development plan through reflection.

Should you have questions about the worksheet, please contact us at mitleadership@mit.edu.

_____________________________________________________________________________

### Part I – Leadership Journey & Values

- What has been your leadership journey up until now?

- In written or visual format share your path up to this point.

- Allow your story to advance in whatever way makes the most sense to you.

- Illustrate 4-6 key moments of strength, challenges faced, transformation, or life lessons.

- What are your core values? (Pick any or all the following questions to answer.)

- What are the core values that you rely upon to make key decisions?

- When you read/look back on your leadership journey, what are the underlying values you relied on when making key decisions?

- Think of the people you most admire. What are the underlying values that you most highly regard about them?

Part II – Identifying Your Vision

- If you were to step into your ideal future self, how would you show up as a leader (in your career, community, personal life, etc.)?

- What is the kind of impact you want to have?

- What behaviors or qualities would you exhibit?

- In what ways would you want to continue growing and learning?

### Part III – Understanding and Reflecting on Data

- x360 Survey: Who did you include as observers? And how many people responded?

- How did your self-perception differ (or not) from that of your other observers? Which charts, graphs, or qualitative feedback illustrated this most for you?

- What were the surprises you found from the report (both positive and negative)? Which charts, graphs, or qualitative feedback illustrated this most for you?

- Additional Data

- What additional feedback have you received that helps you to see yourself more clearly (this could include work reviews, feedback from loved ones, assessments, etc.)?

### Part IV - TO BE DISCUSSED WITH A GUIDE: Action Plan

This next section is a sneak peek into what you can expect to discuss in your guided session.

With a guide and one peer, you will discuss your leadership journey, vision and values and the patterns you have observed from your report and begin to set leadership development goals.

The guided session is facilitated by a trained staff member from Sloan. This session is designed to serve as a foundational building block for both increasing self-awareness and creating a leadership development path that can be accomplished while at Sloan. It is also an opportunity to have meaningful conversations with peers.

Possible Discussion Questions

- What led you to Sloan?

- Share highlights of your leadership journey, values, and/or vision.

- What patterns did you notice from the data?

- What would you like to focus on in the first semester at Sloan?

- What would you like to stop doing?

- What would you like to do instead?

- How will you know if you’re successful?
