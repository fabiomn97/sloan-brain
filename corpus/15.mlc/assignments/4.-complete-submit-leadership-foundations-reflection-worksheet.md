About the Reflection Worksheet for Leadership Foundations

This Reflection Worksheet pulls together your past and present circumstances, along with your desired future, to help you set a focused leadership development path for your time at Sloan. The worksheet asks you to share your story, your values, and your vision. You will use data from your x360 Leadership Development Report and additional feedback you may have available to begin to focus your limited time at Sloan around a leadership development action plan.

Meeting with a Guide

You will submit a completed Reflection Worksheet to Canvas, which we will then review. After that point, you will be assigned a guide. Guides are a group of dedicated staff from across many departments at Sloan who have volunteered their time to serve in this role. All guides have joined us for a training on best facilitation practices for the small group discussions.

Your guide will host a discussion session this summer to dig more deeply into content from your Reflection Worksheet and set action steps. These sessions are designed to help you identify insights and deepen an understanding of your journey, values, vision, along with your strengths and challenges. All of which will reinforce your own leadership development plan.
