## Page 19

Environment Variables are Your Secrets

Why your chatbot works locally but not on the live site

WHAT THEY ARE

WHY THEY'RE NOT ON GITHUB

WHAT TO DO IN VERCEL

Step 11 — Settings → Environment Variables. Add PARLEY_API_KEY and PARLEY_MODEL, then redeploy.

Values your app needs but must never be public — above all the API key that powers your chatbot widget. On your laptop they sit in a .env file.

Claude Code deliberately excludes files with keys in them, so your key never got pushed. That's correct behaviour — not a mistake to fix.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN IT SOUNDS

Anyone who can see your code can use any key that's written into it — on your bill, under your name.

So never paste a key directly into a page or component. Keep it in .env and reference it.

The deploy will go green with the key missing. The site loads, the chatbot just doesn't answer.
