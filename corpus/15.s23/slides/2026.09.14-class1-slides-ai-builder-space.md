## Page 17

SQUADS, SPRINTS & PLANNING DOCS

The structures that hold the semester together

THE SQUAD SYSTEM

HOW A SPRINT RUNS

3–4 projects per squad  your home base for accountability and peer feedback.

Two sprints  roughly three weeks of building each, with reflection at the end.

Max 3 students per project  keeps everyone engaged and hands-on.

Opens with a plan a short planning document, submitted before the sprint starts.

At least 1 solo project  required over the semester, so nobody coasts.

Self-directed building  self-organized squad coordination, optional ondemand tool workshops.

Weekly check-ins  ~2 minutes per project on progress and blockers.

Closes with a check-in  show real progress, get feedback from your squad.

Sprint share-outs  a squad rep brings 2–3 things everyone should see.

Then a share-out  the best builds, tools, and tips reach the whole class.

THE PLANNING DOCUMENT

what you will build  ·  who it is for (a market, a team, or just yourself)  ·  what “done” looks like Short by design, the teaching team reads every one, to help you scope something ambitious but achievable
