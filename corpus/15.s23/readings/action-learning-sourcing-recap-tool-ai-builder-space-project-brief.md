Project Brief 15.S23 AI Builder Space · Project proposal form for Sloan Faculty & Staff · Fall 2026

What this is. AI Builder Space is an MBA proseminar in which students build working AI prototypes during two three-week sprints. The course keeps a short menu of project briefs from Sloan courses, offices, and staff that students can pick up instead of inventing an idea of their own. This form is that brief: students read it to decide whether to take a project on and to start scoping the work.

What to expect. Prototype-grade work from motivated MBAs, most with no technical background — a working thing you can click through and react to, not production software. Students choose their own projects, so this is a pitch rather than a work order.

What we need from you. Fifteen minutes on this form, a 30-minute kickoff chat/zoom, and a look at what they build. Projects with a real user and a responsive sponsor get taken up far more often.

Where to send it. Email the completed form to Rachel Card (card@mit.edu) or Austin van Loon (vanloon@mit.edu) and cc: Mariana Leite (mmleite@mit.edu) by end of day Friday, September 25, 2026. Curated briefs are posted to the course Canvas site with your name and contact before each sprint. Questions welcome — we are glad to help turn a rough idea into something buildable!
