*Posted 2026-09-21 by Mariana Leite*

Hi everyone,

Quick correction on the setup guide for session 2: in Step 3 ("Connect Google Drive to Claude"), the instructions told you to select your "MIT Google account" — that was a mistake!
MIT accounts don't run on Google, so when Google asks you to choose an account, please select your **personal Google account** instead (whichever Gmail/Google account you use day to day).

The guide has already been fixed, so if you haven't reached that step yet, you're all set. If you already went through it and picked an MIT-related account, just redo that step and connect your personal Google account instead.

Sorry for the mix-up, and thanks for bearing with us — see you soon!
