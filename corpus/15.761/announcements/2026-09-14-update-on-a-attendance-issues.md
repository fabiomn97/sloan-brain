*Posted 2026-09-14 by Natasha Patnaik*

Hi Everyone,

We got the following update from Sloan Technology Services, so hopefully no issues with A+ Attendance today. See below:

*We found that some students who are connected to MIT Wi-Fi may still receive the “**Network access restricted**” error because of Apple privacy settings, including iCloud Private Relay.*

*We’ve published a new troubleshooting guide for students with steps to resolve the issue: How to Troubleshoot the “Network Access Restricted” Error in A+ Attendance as a Student (https://mitsloanedtech.mit.edu/support/how-to-troubleshoot-the-network-access-restricted-error-in-a-attendance-as-a-student/)*

*If students have trouble checking in, please first confirm that they’re connected to MIT Wi-Fi and that the MIT VPN or another VPN is not turned on. If they see the “Network access restricted” error, ask them to follow the troubleshooting guide above.*
