*Posted 2026-09-10 by Natasha Patnaik*

Hi Everyone,

We will use A+ Attendance in Canvas to log attendance. We know some students had issues with this new feature yesterday across different classes - so no worries for the first lecture. But starting next class, we will follow the policies below, please read carefully:

**DEFAULT ATTENDANCE POLICY: A+ Attendance in Canvas.**

During class, you’ll check in (i.e. - log your attendance) by entering the attendance code displayed by the teaching team. Make sure you’re connected to MIT Wi-Fi (https://mit.service-now.com/esc?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0034654). You won’t be able to check in if you’re not connected to MIT Wi-Fi. To check in, open A+ Attendance from our Canvas course and enter the attendance code displayed in class. **NOTE:** You will only be able to check in during pre-specified time window at the start of class (12:55PM - 1:15PM). Otherwise, A+ attendance will not allow you to record your attendance, and you will be marked absent.

**BACKUP ATTENDANCE POLICY: Email the TA with subject line "15.761 MM/DD/2026 Attendance", and put the code in body of the email.**

As a backup option (i.e. you have already tried both MIT Guest and MIT Secure, refreshed the page a couple of times, but A+ attendance is still not working for you), you can email me (natashap@mit.edu) with subject line "15.761 MM/DD/2026 Attendance", where MM/DD is the date of the lecture. Put only the attendance code in the body of the email, you do not need to write anything else. **NOTE:** This email must be timestamped between 12:55PM - 1:15PM for us to record your attendance, otherwise you will be marked absent.

**LATE POLICY:** **Don't be late :)**

After initial class announcements, we will put a sign on the door that says “class in session – please do not enter”, and you will be asked to respect it. So be sure to be on time!
