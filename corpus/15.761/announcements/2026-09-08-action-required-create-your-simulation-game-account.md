*Posted 2026-09-08 by Natasha Patnaik*

Hi Everyone,

After you have finalized your team on the google spreadsheet shared in the welcome announcement (also linked here: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ucTKBnRUbuwY469g5opP7n4ZRkzVWU94HqD2bdEjl8Q/edit?usp=sharing ), please follow the steps below to create your simulation game account. **NOTE:** Every individual student should create their own account, but you will play the game together as a team on Sunday, Sept 13. Only create your account after you've finalized your team.

1. Go to the following link: https://op3.responsive.net/lf/levi/
2. Enter your MIT email and click "register".
3. Create and submit a password for your account. **NOTE:** The teaching team can see all of the student passwords, so please don't re-use any of your passwords from other important accounts, or any passwords containing sensitive information.
4. Enter the course code for our class: **go**
5. On the final page, enter the following information and then submit:
  - **Password:** Leave as-is from Step 3
  - **Last Name:** This is just your last name
  - **First Name:** This is just your first name
  - **Section:** Leave blank, do not enter anything
  - **Choose Team:** From the drop-down menu, select your team's assigned name. You can find your team's assigned name in the "team name" column of the same google spreadsheet from earlier (again, linked here: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ucTKBnRUbuwY469g5opP7n4ZRkzVWU94HqD2bdEjl8Q/edit?usp=sharing )

Please complete the above steps by Friday, Sept 11, midnight, and feel free to reach out to the TA (natashap@mit.edu) if you have any questions on the above.
