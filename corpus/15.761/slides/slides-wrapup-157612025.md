## Page 35

Irregular Operations

-  Major disruption (shock) to the steady-state operational conditions of the organization that requires fundamentally different operational strategy and tactics

-  Disruption could include: surge demand, reduced capacity, change in environmental conditions, external/internal damage to systems, …..

-  Many operational failures (cybersecurity included) driven by mismanagement of Irregular Operations events

-  Tradeoffs/tension between steady-state efficiency and what is needed for the management of Irregular Operations
