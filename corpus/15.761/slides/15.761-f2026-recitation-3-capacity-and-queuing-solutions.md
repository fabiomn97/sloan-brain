## Slide 7 — Example 1 – Airline Check-in

- An airline wants to reduce the time it takes to check-in at their counter. The arrival time has mean 10 minutes and standard deviation 10 minutes. The service time is, on average, 15 minutes, with a standard deviation of 5 minutes. They currently have 4 counters serving demand
- The first proposed improvement is to increase from 4 to 5 servers.
- (a)  As a result of this change, will the average server utilization 	increase, decrease, or remain the same?
- (b)  As a result of this change, will the average waiting time in 	queue increase, decrease, or remain unchanged?

## Slide 8 — Example 1 Solution

- This change only increases the number of servers.
- The server utilization must go down, since there will be more servers available.
- The average queue length is decreased – smaller utilization increases the numerator while decreasing the denominator. By Little’s law, L = λ*W, the average waiting time in the queue is also decreased.
- =  / N x 

## Slide 11 — Example 2 – Grocery Store

- Consider your local grocery store with 7 checkout counters, which sees 120 customers per hour on average during a peak period. Checkout for each customer takes 3 minutes on average. What is the average customer waiting time?

## Slide 13 — Example 3 – Car Rental

- Two car rental companies maintain a fleet of 10 SUVs each. The inter-arrival time between requests for an SUV is 4.8 hours, on average, with a standard deviation of 4.8 hours. Assume that, if all SUVs are rented, customers are willing to wait until there is an SUV available. An SUV is rented, on average, for 1 day, with a standard deviation of 1 day.
- The rental companies are considering whether they should pool their fleet or not.
- What is the average time a customer has to wait to rent an SUV without pooling? with pooling?

## Slide 14 — Example 3 Solution

- Without pooling:
- N = 10
- E[A] = 4.8 hours, s[A] = 4.8 hours, CA = s[A] / E[A] = 1
- λ = 1/E[A] = 1/4.8*24=5 customers/day
- E[S] = 1 day, s[S] = 1 day, CS = s[S] / E[S] = 1
- μ = 1/E[S] = 1 customer/day
- ρ = λ / (N*μ) = 5/(10*1) = 0.5
- L = (0.5√2(11))/(1-0.5) * (12 + 12)/2 = 0.0775 customers
- W = L / λ = 0.0775/5 = 0.0155 days = 22.3 minutes
- With pooling:
- N = 20    λ = 10 customers/day   ρ = λ / (N*μ) = 10/(20*1) = 0.5
- L = (0.5√2(21))/(1-0.5) * (12 + 12)/2 = 0.0223 customers
- W = L / λ = 0.0223/10 = 0.00223 days = 3.2 minutes
