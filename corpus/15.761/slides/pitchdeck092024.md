## Page 4

Failing with Operations

-  Jet Blue 2007 Valentine’s Day (Feb 14, 2007 unpredicted snow storm at JFK)

- 1,200 flights (44%) cancelled over one week
- Hundreds of customers were stuck on tarmac for hours
- Over $30M direct financial cost & serious reputation problems

-  Barings Bank (1995)

- 233-year old investment bank collapsed
- One trader (Nick Leeson) lost over $1.4B in unauthorized trading

-  Genzyme (2009-2010)

- Biotech company (very expensive medicines for rare genetic disorders)
- Viral infection in factory imposes loss of sales and penalties of over $200M, led to acquisition by another company
