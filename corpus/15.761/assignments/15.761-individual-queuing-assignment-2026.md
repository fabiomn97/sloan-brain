This assignment contains 3 questions. Questions 1 and 2 refer to the same supermarket, while question 3 is a stand-alone question. You are a budding operations analyst at a supermarket where there are 2 types of checkout lines: self-checkout and regular (personnel-staffed) checkout. Currently, the store has 5 self-checkout machines with a single queue leading up to it, while the regular checkout has 10 attendants, also with a single queue leading up. From historic data, you have found that a customer arrives into the self-checkout line on average every 30 seconds with a standard deviation of 30 seconds, while customers arrive into the regular checkout line on average every 15 seconds with a standard deviation of 15 seconds. In terms of service time, you notice all customers take on average 2 minutes to be serviced, regardless of the checkout line type. However, since some customers may not be familiar with the self-checkout machines, the standard deviation for the self-checkout service times is much higher than that of the regular checkout. The data supports this, as the standard deviation for service times is 4 minutes for self-checkout, but only 3 minutes for regular checkout. Question 1 (40 points): Calculate the average waiting time in seconds for each of the two queues. Please use the same notation as in class (𝜆, 𝜇, 𝜌, 𝑁, 𝐶A, 𝐶S, 𝐿, 𝑊). Question 2 (50 points): The supermarket director has decided that the average waiting time for any customer in the supermarket should be no more than 50 seconds on average. She proposes the following 4 plans:

A. Add another self-checkout machine. B. Add another regular checkout line and hire an additional employee to staff that

checkout line. C. Hire another employee to assist customers at self-checkout. This reduces the

standard deviation of the self-checkout customers from 4 minutes to 2 minutes.

Question 3 (10 points)

Consider two queuing systems with unpredictable (stochastic) arrivals and service times like we discussed in class. We will describe the system with the same notation used in class (𝜆, 𝜇, 𝜌, 𝑁, 𝐶A, 𝐶S, 𝐿, 𝑊).

System I has 1 server while System II has 7 servers. The average inter-arrival of customers is 7 minutes for System I and 1 minute for System II. The average service time is identical for both systems. Finally, the coefficient of variations 𝐶A and 𝐶S, in System I are each 25% of those in System II.

Determine which of the two systems has a smaller average waiting time in each of the following scenarios:

A. The utilization factor (𝜌) of System I is higher than 0.68. B. The utilization factor (𝜌) of System I is lower than 0.65.

Hint: You don’t have enough information to calculate the exact average waiting time (𝑊) in each scenario, but you can compare the values of 𝜆, 𝜇, 𝜌, 𝑁, 𝐶A, 𝐶S,… etc between the

two systems and this might tell you about how the average wait times (𝑊) compare between these two systems.
