15.761 Assignment 1: JetBlue Deicing

Please keep your responses concise and short!

Case synopsis

JetBlue operates from 11 gates in Logan Airport, and the at-gate deicing capacity is

based on 4 teams of 3 deicers, each that can process 4 airplanes each hour (each

team processes 1 plane per hour) to support 59 departures a day over 18 hours (see

Exhibit 5).

Based on the JetBlue Deicing case, please respond to the following questions:

1. (20 points) Considering the deicing capacity and the average flight load over

the 18-hour operations. What is the utilization of the deicing capacity?

2. (40 points) The attached excel file (see also graph below) uses the flight data

from Exhibit 5 in the case to graph the following build-up diagram of the

planes around the gates. It assumes that  all departures times are kept with

certainty and that the deicing processing time is deterministic.
