- A Glimpse into EV Battery Quality Control
- [optional] Challenges and opportunities for high-quality battery production at scale
- [optional] Where will be the next electric vehicle superpower? (https://www.economist.com/finance-and-economics/2025/03/18/where-will-be-the-next-electric-vehicle-superpower) (To obtain access to this article, please sign in via the MIT Libraries Page (https://libguides.mit.edu/news/economist))

**Questions:**

1. Estimate the rate of defective battery packs the Glimpse solution is capable of detecting today. Are you surprised by this rate? What level of quality control is ‘good enough’ when uncaught defects can lead to safety risks and billion-dollar recalls?
2. How much would Glimpse's customers (e.g. EV manufacturers) be willing to pay for their software subscription?
3. How is Glimpse leveraging AI to 1) improve its technology and 2) build a competitive moat? To what extent is this competitive advantage defensible?
