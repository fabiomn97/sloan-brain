**Case:** Data Science at Target

**Questions:**

1. What are the challenges Desai is facing when he is appointed as the VP of Business Intelligence at Target.com?
2. What are the investments and organizational capabilities that Target.com needs to develop in order to implement successful AI-enabled intelligent processes and systems?
3. How different are the challenges that Desai is facing when appointed as Chief Data and Analytics Officer of the Target Corporation?
4. What are the additional challenges that exist when implementing AI-enabled intelligent processes and systems within the entire Target enterprise?
