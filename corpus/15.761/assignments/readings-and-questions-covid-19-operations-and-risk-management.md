**All students:** View the COVID-19 Data Explorer (https://ourworldindata.org/coronavirus).

**Students with** **last names beginning with A-L:** Read paper 1, prepare the paper 1 questions, and skim paper 2.

**Students with last names beginning with M-Z:** Read paper 2, prepare the paper 2 questions, and skim paper 1.

**Readings:**

- Paper 1: Age-stratified infection fatality rate of COVID-19 in the non-elderly population
- Paper 2: Serious adverse events of special interest following mRNA COVID-19 vaccination in randomized trials in adults

**Paper 1 Questions:**

1. What metrics should guide the government management of a pandemic such as COVID-19?
2. Which of the following countries, US, UK, Germany and Sweden managed the COVID pandemic better? (you can add other countries who feel did relatively well!)

**Paper 2 Questions:**

1. What are relevant metrics to evaluate the risk-benefits of the COVID-19 vaccines?
2. How well can they be measured?
