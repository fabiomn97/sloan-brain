15.761 – HP Individual Assignment

Please keep your responses concise and short!

Consider the attached excel file with monthly demand information on the 6 European

models (options) the HP printers. Assume that the daily and therefore the monthly

demand is Normally distributed, and that there are 30 days in a month:

1. (50 pts) Build and describe an Excel model to recommend quantitative target

inventory levels (up-to-S policy), for each of the 6 European options,

assuming a weekly (periodic review) replenishment and a 98% service level

(k=2.054) for the following two scenarios: (i) 5-week sea shipment lead-time;

(ii) 3-day air shipment lead-time. Evaluate the total supply-chain inventory

levels (safety stock, cycle stock, pipeline stock) that will result from the

weekly target inventory base-stock levels that you recommend for these

options.

2. (25 pts) For option AA, compute and compare the total inventory and

transportation cost of the two scenarios in 1 above (sea vs. air shipping),

assuming the cost of goods for each printer is $528, inventory holding cost

rate is of 50% per year, sea transportation costs of $1 per printer (lead-time 5

weeks) and air transportation costs of $11 per printer (lead-time is 3 days).

Reminder: the total average inventory level under an up-to-S policy is equal

to S – E[DO RP]/2.

3. (25 pts) Suppose that the European options could be specialized on demand

(i.e., their inventory could be pooled). Calculate the optimal inventory target

level under this scenario and compare how the safety stock under this scenario

compares with your answer to 1 above.
