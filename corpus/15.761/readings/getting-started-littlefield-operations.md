Getting Started with Littlefield Technologies

WELCOME

Littlefield is an online simulation where you manage the operations of a factory. You access your factory by logging into a website with an id and password that were previously registered. Your instructor enables access to the simulator and determines when it will run. Your objective is make the most money over the life of the factory while it is running by making changes to Littlefield's operations.

Read the other sections in this "Getting Started" document important information about how your factory operates.

OPERATIONS OVERVIEW

Littlefield Technologies (LT) is a state-of-the-art highly automated factory producing digital satellite receivers. Orders for custom receivers are received from customers. Once an order is accepted, raw materials are pulled from the materials buffer in the form of kits and matched with the order. The order becomes a job and is released to the shop floor. Materials in the form of kits are purchased from a reliable supplier.

The kits then go through a four-step process carried out at three stations named , and board stuffing, testing . In the first step, components are combined at board stuffing to build each receiver according the tuning customer's specifications. The receivers then undergo a test at the testing station and then tuning at the tuning station. Finally the receivers undergo a final test and are sent to the customer. Payment is received from the customer as soon as the order is shipped.

You will have some cash on hand when the assignment begins. This amount is depleted by both capacity and inventory purchases. Revenues earned from filled orders increase the cash balance. Cash held earns interest, compounded every simulated day. There are no taxes, salaries, nor fixed overhead costs to consider. To reduce chances of insolvency, you are not allowed to purchase new machines if the resulting cash balance would prevent the next inventory order at the current number of kits.

Raw materials inventory is purchased from a reliable supplier. Payment is made as each order is placed. Materials have a fixed lead time for delivery.

Littlefield competes by promising aggressive lead times to customers, along with refunds in the form of price reductions if the lead time promises are not met.

REGISTERING

Every student must register to access the simulation. To register for the simulation, set your browser to the address provided by your instructor and click the "Register" button. You will be prompted to enter an id and password that you will use will use in the future to log into the game and identify yourself to your teammates and professor. Your id is different from your team name. You will be asked for your team name later. The id and password may only consist of numbers, lower-case letters, and the _ and @ symbols.

You will then be presented with a form asking for additional information. Fill it in and submit it.
