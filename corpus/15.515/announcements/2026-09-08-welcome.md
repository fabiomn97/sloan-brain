*Posted 2026-09-08 by Chloe L. Xie*

Hello Students!

Welcome to MIT! And happy first day of school! For many of you, this might be the last, true, ‘first day of school’! I get quite sentimental thinking about it! I hope it’s going brilliantly. 🥹

I am looking forward to meeting you on Thursday morning in your introductory accounting class. The first 60 minutes of class on Thursday we will dive straight into a very short **mini case** titled - The National Hockey League’s 2020 Collective Bargaining Agreement (NHL CBA). We will leave a little bit of time in the end for taking care of some **administrative items**.

The case can be found on the Canvas site for this class in the PRE CLASS - Session 1 Readings link (which should be on the home page of the course website). Please read the case before class and focus on the seven items that are queued up for discussion (each item is bolded). The topic we want you to be prepared to discuss in class is *when* do you think the team should report revenue or expenses for a representative transaction in each category. For example, if a fan purchased a season ticket at the beginning of the season, *when* do you think the team should report the funds for the sale as revenue, and why?

One more housekeeping item – relating to **class attendance**. We’ll be using A+ Attendance in this course. In the first five minutes of class, you’ll check in by entering the attendance code displayed by the teaching team. You can download the Canvas Student app (phone) or log in to Canvas in a web browser (tablet). My understanding is that you must be connected to MIT Wi-Fi to check in. (Sloan Tech instructions on using A+ Attendance (https://mitsloanedtech.mit.edu/support/how-to-check-into-aplus-attendance-using-a-unique-code-as-a-student/))

See you in two days! Enjoy this beautiful weather - campus is never better.

Warmly,

Chloe
