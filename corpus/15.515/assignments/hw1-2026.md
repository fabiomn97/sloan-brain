HW Set 1 Balance Sheet Equation (BSE) Due on September 21, 2026 at 4pm

Background: Flick Pest Control LLC (“FPC”) is a full-service residential pest control company providing services to prevent and exterminate termites, bed bugs, ants and rodents. FPC was founded in 1986 by Bob and Fran Flick and has proudly served the communities of Cambridge, MA, for over 35 years. In 2021, Annie acquired the business from Bob and Fran via a search fund after graduating from MIT’s Sloan School of Business. After beginning her career in consulting, Annie had an itch to do something more entrepreneurial, but she struggled on how to pursue entrepreneurship as she had no startup ideas and was worried about the risk profile of early-stage ventures. At Sloan, she discovered the Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (“ETA”) Club and learned about search funds – the entrepreneurial path of identifying, acquiring and operating an existing, enduringly profitable business. To learn more about ETA, Annie leveraged the Sloan community to network with ten active search fund entrepreneurs who were at different stages of the process – from fundraising to searching to operating. She ultimately decided that ETA was the perfect path for her and during spring of her second year, Annie raised capital from institutional search fund investors to cover a salary, travel and deal expenses for up to 24 months while she searched for an attractive business. It took her nine months of searching but she discovered FPC with the help of her investors and acquired the business in 2021. Annie was now Owner and CEO of her own business, living out her entrepreneurial dream of being her own boss and leading a team. Upon entering FPC, Annie had to get up to speed on several areas of the business, including accounting which was relatively new to her. She decided that during the first year of ownership that she would personally handle the accounting in order to gain a better hold of the function and ensure accuracy. (If you’re interested in learning more about ETA, consider joining Sloan’s Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition Club which hosts weekly informational events.) Instructions: Below we provide the balance sheet for the period ending December 31, 2021 for FPC. On the next page is a list of transactions the firm engaged in during the first 6 months of fiscal year 2022. Please record those transactions using the Balance Sheet Equation in a template similar to what we use in class, and prepare an income statement and balance sheet for the period ending 6/30/2022.
