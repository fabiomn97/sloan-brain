List of Accounting Synonyms

- Allowance for doubtful accounts, allowance for bad debts, allowance for uncollectible accounts

- Bad Debt Expense, provision for doubtful accounts, provision for bad debts, provision for uncollectible accounts, uncollectable accounts expense, doubtful accounts expense.

- Sales, revenue

- Deferred revenue, unearned revenue, accrued revenue

- Gross margin, gross profit margin, gross profit  (though note margin is often expressed in % whereas profit is in $)

- Shareholder’s equity, stockholder’s equity

- Net income, earnings, profits, income

- LIFO reserve, revaluation to LIFO, excess of FIFO cost over LIFO cost, LIFO allowance

- Salvage value, residual value

- Receivables, accounts receivable, notes receivable (same idea)

- Bonds payable, notes payable, debentures

- Accrued wages, wage payable, salary payable
