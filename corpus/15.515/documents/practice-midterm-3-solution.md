(b) (4 Points) As of the end of 2017, how much has Harley-Davidson reduced its income taxes payable over the life of its operations by using LIFO instead of FIFO? Assume a constant tax rate of 30%.

Harley has increased its COGS by using the LIFO method. The LIFO reserve reflects the cumulative additional COGS that has been recognized (thus lowering taxable income). Thus,

Tax Savings = Incremental Cost * Tax Rate

X        =       52,355K         *        .30

X       =       15,707K

(c) (4 points) What would Harley-Davidson’s cost of goods sold have been if they had used FIFO for 2017?

COGSFIFO = COGSLIFO – Change in LIFO Reserve

Change in LIFO Reserve = 52,355K – 48,267K = 4,088K

COGSFIFO = 3,261,683K – 4,088K

COGSFIFO = 3,257,595K

(d) (8 points) Suppose instead that Harley converted to FIFO on December 31, 2016 and continued to use FIFO for all of 2017. For 2017, what would the following amounts be? For this part only, assume that Harley-Davidson’s tax rate on the difference between LIFO and FIFO was 0.0%.

Net income for 2017				_____525,847K _____

Shareholders’ equity on December 31, 2017	_____1,896,632K_____

Operating Cash Flows for 2017:			_____no change_____

From above, the change in Change in LIFO Reserve is 4,088K, reducing COGS, so 2017 net income increases by 4,088K to 525,847K.

However, the full amount of the LIFO reserve increases Shareholders’ equity (part as of December 31, 2016 and the remainder during the year December 31, 2017). To see the first part, note that inventories as of December 31, 2016 increase by the amount of reserve, and to balance the BSE, RE (SE) increase.

SE = 52,355 + 1,844,277 = 1,896,632

Since there is no cash tax effect, OCF are unchanged.

Lengthening the useful life will reduce the straight-line depreciation expense each year resulting in higher net income. Because depreciation expense is non-cash, it will not affect operating cash flows. Both the numerator and denominator for ROE will increase by equal amounts - since this ratio is less than 1, it will result in an increase.
