2. Marketable Securities — Northstar Systems

Northstar Systems, Inc. invests excess cash in debt and equity securities and prepares its financial statements under U.S. GAAP. Ignore income taxes. For these questions, AFS debt securities report unrealized gains and losses in OCI; trading debt securities and equity securities report fair-value changes in net income.

Investment A — Available-for-Sale Debt Security

On January 1, 2026, Northstar purchases Cedar Corporation bonds for $800,000 cash. The bonds pay 4% annual interest each December 31 and are classified as available-for-sale. On December 31, 2026, Northstar receives the annual interest payment and the bonds have a fair value of $860,000.

Investment B — Trading Debt Security

On January 1, 2026, Northstar purchases Glacier Corporation bonds for $1,200,000 cash. The bonds pay 5% annual interest each December 31 and are classified as trading securities. On December 31, 2026, Northstar receives the annual interest payment and the bonds have a fair value of $1,125,000.

Investment C — Equity Security

On July 1, 2026, Northstar purchases 30,000 shares of Horizon Inc. common stock for $18 per share. During 2026, Horizon pays Northstar a cash dividend of $0.40 per share. On December 31, 2026, Horizon shares trade for $21 per share.

9. What amount of interest income does Northstar recognize from the Cedar bonds in 2026? A. $32,000 B. $24,000 C. $60,000 D. $34,400
