Financial Accounting (15.515) Self-Assessment Problem Set 1 Instructions: The following ten multiple choice questions are designed to provide you with an opportunity to assess how well you understand the material we have covered to date.  You do not have to submit a solution to these questions, they will not be graded, and you can use AI if it helps you ensure you understand the material.  There will be at least one correct answer to each multiple-choice question. On the exam, you would need to get all of them correct to receive credit for the question.

[A]   Unearned Revenue On January 1, a customer pays a company $7,200 in advance for six months of services. By January 31, the company has completed one month of service. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. On January 1, total assets increase by $7,200. 2. On January 1, shareholders’ equity increases by $7,200. 3. On January 31, liabilities decrease by $1,200. 4. On January 31, shareholders’ equity increases by $1,200. 5. None of the above.

[B]   Accounts Receivable A company performs $4,600 of services for customers on account. Which statement(s) is/are correct at the time the services are performed? 1. Cash increases by $4,600. 2. Accounts receivable increases by $4,600. 3. Revenue increases by $4,600. 4. Total shareholders’ equity increases by $4,600. 5. None of the above.

[C]   Collection of Receivables A company collects $3,000 from customers’ outstanding accounts receivable. The collection will: 1. Increase total assets by $3,000. 2. Decrease accounts receivable by $3,000. 3. Increase cash by $3,000. 4. Increase revenue by $3,000. 5. None of the above.

[D]   Prepaid Expenses On April 20, a company pays a contractor $900 in advance for maintenance services to be provided in May. Which statement(s) is/are correct on April 20? 1. Cash decreases by $900. 2. An asset other than cash increases by $900. 3. An expense of $900 is recognized. 4. Total assets decrease by $900. 5. None of the above.

[E]   Accrued Expenses On March 31, a company recognizes $1,100 of salaries earned by employees but not yet paid. The company should: 1. Record a liability. 2. Decrease cash. 3. Recognize an expense. 4. Decrease shareholders’ equity. 5. None of the above.

[F]   Inventory and Cost of Goods Sold A company sells 400 units of inventory on account. Each unit originally cost $12 and the company charges customers a 100% markup over cost. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. Sales revenue is $9,600. 2. Cost of goods sold is $4,800. 3. Accounts receivable increases by $9,600.

[G]   Accounts Payable A company purchases $3,600 of inventory on account and later pays $3,000 to the supplier. Considering both transactions together, which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. Inventory increases by $3,600. 2. Cash decreases by $3,000. 3. Accounts payable increases by a net $600. 4. Shareholders’ equity decreases by $3,000. 5. None of the above.

[H]   Prepaid Insurance On December 31, 2021, a company pays $7,200 for a one-year insurance policy covering 2022. On June 30, 2022, it records six months of insurance usage. Which statement(s) is/are correct about the June 30 adjustment? 1. Prepaid insurance decreases by $3,600. 2. Insurance expense increases by $3,600. 3. Cash decreases by $3,600. 4. Shareholders’ equity decreases by $3,600. 5. None of the above.

[I]   Interest Accrual A company has a $9,000 loan outstanding on January 1. The loan bears 8% annual interest. On June 30, the company accrues six months of interest using the beginning-of-year loan balance and does not compound interest. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. Interest expense is $360. 2. Interest payable increases by $360. 3. Cash decreases by $360. 4. Shareholders’ equity decreases by $360. 5. None of the above.

[J]   Loan Principal A company pays $3,000 of loan principal in cash. The payment will: 1. Decrease cash by $3,000. 2. Decrease the loan payable by $3,000. 3. Decrease interest expense by $3,000. 4. Decrease total liabilities by $3,000. 5. None of the above.
