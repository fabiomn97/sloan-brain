Financial Accounting (15.515)

Self-Assessment Problem Set 2

Instructions: The following ten multiple choice questions are designed to provide you with an opportunity to assess how well you understand the material we have covered to date. You do not have to submit a solution to these questions, they will not be graded, and you can use AI if it helps you ensure you understand the material. There will be at least one correct answer to each multiple-choice question. On the exam, you would need to get all of them correct to receive credit for the question.

[A]   Billing and Revenue Recognition

A company bills a customer for services before performing any of the services. The customer has not yet paid. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. The company may record Accounts Receivable even though it has not yet recognized revenue. 2. The company may record Deferred Revenue even though it has not yet received cash. 3. Revenue must be recognized because an invoice has been sent to the customer. 4. The transaction can increase both an asset and a liability without affecting shareholders’ equity. 5. None of the above.

[B]   Revenue Recognition and Cash Collection

Which statement(s) about the timing of revenue recognition and cash collection is/are correct? 1. Revenue can be recognized before cash is collected. 2. Cash can be collected before revenue is recognized. 3. Revenue recognition always increases cash or Accounts Receivable on the date revenue is recognized. 4. The timing of cash collection, by itself, determines when revenue is recognized. 5. None of the above.

[C]   Seller and Customer Accounting A customer pays in advance for equipment that the seller will deliver next month. The seller has not yet satisfied its performance obligation. Which statement(s) is/are correct immediately after the payment? 1. The seller records a liability. 2. The customer may record an asset representing its advance payment. 3. The seller records revenue because cash has been received. 4. The payment can increase the seller’s assets without increasing the seller’s shareholders’ equity. 5. None of the above.

[D]   Product Sale A company ships inventory to a customer FOB shipping point (meaning control of the goods transfers to the customer when the seller ships them) and bills the customer. The customer has not yet paid. Which statement(s) is/are correct for the seller? 1. Revenue can be recognized when the goods are shipped. 2. Accounts Receivable can increase even though cash has not been collected. 3. The reduction in inventory and recognition of cost of goods sold reduce shareholders’ equity. 4. The increase in Accounts Receivable and decrease in Inventory must be equal. 5. None of the above.

[E]   Accounts Payable

A company purchases inventory on account and later pays the supplier. Considering the purchase and the subsequent payment, which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. The purchase increases both an asset and a liability. 2. The subsequent payment decreases both an asset and a liability. 3. Paying the supplier reduces expense in the period of payment. 4. Neither transaction, by itself, must affect shareholders’ equity.

[F]   Bad Debt Expense

A company increases its estimate of uncollectible accounts and records additional bad debt expense under the allowance method. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. The Allowance for Doubtful Accounts increases. 2. Net Accounts Receivable decreases. 3. Net income decreases. 4. Gross Accounts Receivable decreases. 5. None of the above.

[G]   Write-Off of an Account Receivable

A company using the allowance method writes off a customer account that it has determined will not be collected. Assume the allowance account has a sufficient balance. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. Gross Accounts Receivable decreases. 2. The Allowance for Doubtful Accounts decreases. 3. Net Accounts Receivable decreases. 4. Bad debt expense increases on the date of the write-off. 5. None of the above.

[H]   Allowance Reversal

A company concludes that its prior estimate of uncollectible receivables was too pessimistic and records a reversal of previously recognized bad debt expense. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. The Allowance for Doubtful Accounts decreases. 2. Net Accounts Receivable increases. 3. Net income increases. 4. Gross Accounts Receivable increases. 5. None of the above.

[I]   Receivables Analysis A firm’s sales grow substantially while ending gross Accounts Receivable remains nearly unchanged. Holding other factors constant, which statement(s) is/are generally correct? 1. Accounts Receivable turnover increases. 2. Days receivable decreases. 3. The pattern is consistent with faster collection of receivables. 4. The pattern necessarily proves that the firm has become less aggressive in recognizing revenue. 5. None of the above.

[J]   Deferred Revenue

A company begins the year with a Deferred Revenue balance. During the year it receives additional customer advances and also performs services related to amounts previously deferred. Which statement(s) is/are correct? 1. Customer advances can increase Deferred Revenue. 2. Performing services related to amounts previously deferred can decrease Deferred Revenue and increase revenue. 3. A decrease in Deferred Revenue caused by recognizing revenue increases shareholders’ equity. 4. The ending Deferred Revenue balance must be zero once any revenue has been recognized from the account. 5. None of the above.
