Zero-Coupon Bond

On January 1, 2026, Alpine Corp. issues a 2-year zero-coupon bond with a face value of $1,210,000. The bond pays no periodic cash interest and matures on December 31, 2027. The market interest rate at issuance is 10%. Alpine prepares financial statements on December 31.

1. What amount of cash does Alpine receive when it issues the bond on January 1, 2026? A. $1,210,000 B. $1,100,000 C. $1,000,000 D. $990,000

2. Which Balance Sheet Equation effect best describes the bond issuance on January 1, 2026? A. Assets increase $1,000,000 and liabilities increase $1,000,000. B. Assets increase $1,210,000 and liabilities increase $1,210,000. C. Assets increase $1,000,000 and stockholders’ equity increases $1,000,000. D. Assets are unchanged and liabilities increase $1,210,000.

3. How much interest expense should Alpine recognize for 2026? A. $121,000 B. $100,000 C. $110,000 D. $0

4. What is the carrying value of the bond immediately after Alpine records 2026 interest expense? A. $1,000,000 B. $1,210,000 C. $1,100,000 D. $1,110,000

5. How much interest expense should Alpine recognize for 2027 before repaying the bond at maturity? A. $100,000 B. $121,000 C. $110,000 D. $0
