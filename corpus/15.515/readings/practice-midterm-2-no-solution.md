Question 5 – True or False (30 points total; 3 points each); circle one

A. (True or False) Under the allowance method, total assets are reduced when an accounts

receivable is written-off.

B. (True or False) The ending balance in Allowance for Uncollectible Accounts represents the

amount a company thinks it will not collect from credit sales made to customers during this period.

C.  (True or False) When using the FIFO inventory method in periods of declining prices, upon

the sale of inventory quantities, lower cost units are transferred from the balance sheet when a sale is recorded, resulting in higher gross profit.

D. (True or False) All else equal, in periods of declining prices, a company that uses FIFO

inventory costing reports a higher ending inventory balance compared to the ending inventory balance that it would have reported if it used LIFO.

E. (True or False) All else equal, the ending inventory of companies using LIFO should be

cheaper to insure or replace than the inventory of companies using FIFO.

F. (True or False) If a company decreases the salvage value of an asset without changing its

useful life, depreciation expense will increase in the next year.

G. (True or False) When Company A purchases Company B, and determines that the fair value

of Company B’s assets are greater than the book value of the assets, it will definitely recognize some goodwill as part of the transaction.

H. (True or False) Unearned revenue reflects liabilities to a firm’s customers for goods and

services that must be delivered in the next year.

I.  (True or False) When a company recognizes a goodwill impairment, its liabilities will be

increased, and its shareholders equity will be reduced.

J. (True or False) A sale of a Plant, Property, and Equipment (PP&E) asset at more than its

net book value requires that a company recognize a gain in the income statement.
