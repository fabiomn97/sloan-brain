- Is Research and Development (R&D) an asset?
- Asset Definition:   An asset is a present right of an entity to an economic benefit (FASB Conceptual Framework).

## Slide 6 — Research & development (FASB)

- 6
- The FASB indicates that many activities, like research and development, are undertaken with the expectation of obtaining an economic benefit in the future.
- The costs incurred in those activities are not assets, because assessments of when a present right exists and whether the right is to a related economic benefit may be especially uncertain.
- Does the FASB consider R&D an asset?

## Slide 30 — Differences between u.S. GAAp and IFRS

- 30
- US GAAP
  - Published in multiple volumes over nine inches thick
  - Rules and interpretations with industry-specific guidance
  - Limited use of fair value
  - More conservative
  - Each interim period viewed as integral part of annual period
- IFRS
  - Published in single volume about two inches thick
  - Principles with no industry specific guidance
  - Broad use of fair value
  - Less conservative
  - Each interim period viewed as discrete reporting period
