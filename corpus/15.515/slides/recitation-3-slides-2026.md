## Slide 5 — The LIFO Reserve: Converting from LIFO to FIFO

- Accounting standards require that companies that report using LIFO provide the LIFO Reserve, which can be used to convert the financial statements to FIFO.
- To find the ending inventory balance under FIFO, simply add the LIFO reserve to the ending inventory balance under LIFO.
- To find COGS under FIFO, subtract the change in the LIFO reserve since last period from COGS under LIFO.

## Slide 10

    - Cost of Asset: expenditure necessary to ready the asset for intended use
    - Estimated Salvage (Residual) Value: predicted selling price of long-lived asset less any removal costs at the end of useful life
- Depreciable cost: Asset Cost - Salvage value
- Useful Life: period over which the asset is expected to provide economic benefits to the firm
- Depreciation expense: The amount of the asset’s cost allocated to this period’s income statement
- Accumulated depreciation: The cumulative amount of depreciation expenses taken over the asset’s useful life
    - A contra asset
- Book value: Asset’s remaining unallocated cost  historical cost less accumulated depreciation
- General Terminology - Depreciation

## Slide 11 — Estimating Depreciation Expense

- 11
- The “Straight Line Method”
- Depreciation expense per year =
- (Acquisition Cost – Salvage Value)
- Estimated Useful Life
- Estimated Useful Life - period over which the asset is expected to provide economic benefits to the firm
  - Not the same as physical life.
- Salvage Value – Expected realizable value of asset at end of useful life.
- Replace with Net Book Value when calculating a new Depreciation Expense after purchase

## Slide 16 — Gain or Loss on Disposal of PPE

- 16
- Procedure for disposal of PPE
- 1.  Record cash or the market value of the asset received for the sold PPE
- 2.  Record the disposal of the asset by removing the cost of the asset from PPE
- 3.  Reverse or offset the accumulated depreciation associated with the asset
- 4.  Calculate gain or loss as follows:
- Cash – (Cost - AccDep) = Gain/Loss
