## Slide 1 — Let’s Review

- You’ve graduated from Sloan and your new startup has just landed its first big client. You agree on a 1-year service agreement with a sales price of $1 million. The agreement has the following terms:
- January 1, 2029: Your client pays an advance of $500,000.
- January 2, 2029 - December 31, 2029: You perform services for your client.
- January 1, 2030: Your client pays you the remaining $500,000.
- Goals:
- Help you practice for the exam!
- Understand the mechanics of revenue-related accounts.

**Speaker notes:** Emphasize to students that they are not going to be making the actual entries – but they need to understand how individual events affect the balances.
