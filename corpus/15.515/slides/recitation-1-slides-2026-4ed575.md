## Slide 4 — Three important equations

  - Assets  =  Liabilities  +  Stockholders’ Equity
  - Net Income  =  Revenues  -  Expenses  (+ Gains – Losses)
  - End. Ret. Earn.  =  Beg. Ret. Earn. + Net income  -  		   Dividends

## Slide 44 — Distinguish between current and non-current accounts

- Current assets are expected to be realized/consumed/sold within one fiscal year
- Examples for current/non-current accounts:
- Assets
- Current Assets
  - Cash
  - A/R
  - Inventories
- Non-current Assets
  - PPE
  - Intangible Assets
- Liabilities
- Current Liabilities
  - A/P
  - Accrued Liabilities
  - Wages Payable (could also be non-current)
- Non-current Liabilities
  - Long-Term Debt (e.g., bank loans)
  - Deferred Taxes
- Shareholder’s Equity
- Common Stock
- Retained Earnings
