**Class 3 - Structure: Key Takeaways**

Structural choices can

1. Dramatically increase clarity and retention for your audience
2. Ensure your argument is well-supported and compelling to your audience

 Remember these key considerations in building persuasive message structure:

1. Direct/indirect structure
2. The Shield framework
3. Introductions (Hook, essential context, recommendation)
4. Body (Supporting points, evidence, transitions, one vs. two-sided arguments)
5. Conclusions (Recap, next step, final statement)
