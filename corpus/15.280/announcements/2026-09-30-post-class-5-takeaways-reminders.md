**Class 5 - Delivery + Q&A: Key Takeaways**

Delivery and Q&A are about making deliberate choices.

- Strive for dynamism and congruence in your verbal and non-verbal delivery choices
- Think of Q&A as an opportunity that you can prepare for: take control, paraphrase (reframe if needed), and reinforce your message
  - Take advantage of the memory curve - reinforce your main point after the Q&A as well
