**Class 2: Giving & Receiving Feedback - Key Takeaways**

*Giving Feedback - tools to consider*

- Pause on the ladder of inference
- Use specific **observations + impact** to structure your feedback

*Receiving Feedback - tools to consider*

- Listen to understand
- Consider clarifying questions if needed, paraphrasing

*Consider culture and audience when delivering feedback*

- Direct vs. indirect negative feedback
- Upgraders/downgraders
- Ratio of positive to negative feedback

*Remember the intention behind the feedback.*
