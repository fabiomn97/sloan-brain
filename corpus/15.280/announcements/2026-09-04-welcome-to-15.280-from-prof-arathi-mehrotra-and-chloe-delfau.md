**Class #1: Intro + Communication Strategy**

Our **syllabus** is posted on our Canvas site—please read it before class to get a sense of what to expect this semester.

Information on required readings (https://canvas.mit.edu/courses/38561/assignments/487027?module_item_id=1626621) for Class #1 is on Canvas. Here are a few questions for you to think about as you read the **DRW Technologies case**—no need to submit any responses. These questions are simply a way for you to think about the case in the context of a communication strategy framework.

1. Communicator-Audience Relationship
  - *What was Claiborne’s objective in writing the email to the procurement managers? What is his credibility?*
  - *What does Claiborne know about the procurement managers? How does this shape his communication?*

2. Message - Creation, Adaptation, Delivery
  - *What message has Claiborne sent to the executives and what do you take away from his words, tone, and structure?*
  - *What other communication channels might Claiborne have used here, and why?*
  - *What do we know about DRW Technologies that may have shaped the reaction to Claiborne’s communication?*

3. What next?
  - *Imagine you find yourself where Claiborne is at the end of the case. What would be the first next step in your communication strategy? Who would you reach out to? How? What would you say?*
  - *What might the impact of AI be on the Communicator-Audience relationship and planning the message?*
