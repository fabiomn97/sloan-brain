**Key Takeaways from Class 1**

To develop an effective communication strategy:

1. Analyze the Communicator-Audience Relationship
  - What is your credibility? What is the level of trust between you and your audience?
  - Who is your audience and what is important to them? What are their goals? How do they feel about the topic?
  - Is your objective specific, tangible, and reasonable?

2. Develop your message

-
  - Creation
    - Content - what arguments, facts, data, examples, and/or stories will you include? What is the WIIFT for the audience?
    - Structure - what structural choices will you make? (direct/indirect, intro/conclusions/transitions, one vs two-sided arguments)

  - Adaptation
    - Channel - what is the optimal channel based on audience needs?
    - Culture - what cultural nuances should you consider? (geographic, organizational, team, situational context)

  - Delivery
    - Style - what choices will you make regarding tone, verbal/non-verbal delivery, word choice?
    - Visual aids - will you use slides or other aids to help you make your points?

*Bottom line: Develop a clear strategy before you communicate to get the response that you want.*
