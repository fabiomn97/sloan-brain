## Page 2

MESSAGE   STRATEGY

Using direct message structure, how will you preview (BLUF) your objective: Acknowledging counter argument/ downside/risk is key to building credibility, how will you do this?

What evidence will you use to support your argument? (e.g. story, data)

What persuasive tactics (e.g. Cialdini’s principles) will you employ?

DELIVERY STRATEGY

What will you include in your introduction to grab the audience’s attention? What will you say to prepare the audience to hear your message? Every team member must have a speaking role, how will transitions be managed between team members?

How will you close presentation and invite questions? How will you close Q&A? How will team members position themselves when NOT speaking, i.e. will you sit or stand?

Q&A

What is your team’s plan for managing Q&A? What questions/objections do you expect from your audience? 1. 2. 3. 4. 5.

VISUALS   OPTIONAL

If used, how will the slides contribute to the persuasive element of your presentation? How will you/your team speakers interact with the PowerPoint slides to ensure focus remains on the audience? REHEARSAL PLAN

What is your team’s plan to ensure a cohesive and professional delivery of presentation?
