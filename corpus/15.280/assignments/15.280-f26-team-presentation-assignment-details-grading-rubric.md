15.280 COMMUNICATION FOR LEADERS

Team Persuasive Presentation Preliminary Client Recommendation

[10% of Grade  – One Grade for Team]

Based on the you organization studied for your 15.311 team project, each team will prepare an oral persuasive presentation for 15.280 Communication for Leaders in addition to the written report for 15.311 Organizational Process (OP). Although the Team Presentation is based on the organization you are studying, it is not a presentation of your OP Report. Rather, this is a unique assignment that asks you to present one recommendation to your client to improve the organization. Similar to many work situations, your performance—and thus your team’s grade—may depend on factors outside of your control. Therefore, success with this  assignment will depend on the ability of your team to make strategic choices about your process which, no doubt, will impact the final deliverable.

ASSIGNMENT SCENARIO

You and your team mates have been researching and analyzing an organization for your OP report. Although the client company understands this report is an analysis of their organization, they want to take advantage of your team’s insight to help them improve as an organization. They have asked you to identify ONE change you would recommend based on your research.

The client has asked that the team’s presentation take place on Tuesday, December 1 and for the team to deliver a 15 minute persuasive presentation that includes 5 minutes of Q&A and previews the team’s one key recommendation.

ASSIGNMENT DETAILS

WHEN: Tuesday, December 1 during class or in an extra class session that will be scheduled to accommodate all Team Presentations. TIMING

-  1-Minute Context Setting: The team will have 1 minute to describe the context of the situation and provide a brief profile of the client/audience for your Professor, TA and your classmates. (This 1 minute does not count toward the 15 minutes for the presentation itself.)
-  10-Minutes Persuasive Presentation of key recommendation, reasoning and evidence
-  5-Minutes Q&A DELIVERABLES At the time of your presentation, please post the following information to Canvas:

-  Completed Planner, available on Canvas in the Team Presentation folder
-  Visuals if used

STRATEGIC CHOICES FOR THE TEAM PERSUASIVE PRESENTATION

You will need to be strategic in all your choices and particularly with the following:

-  Selecting what you will present as the recommendation and supporting evidence within the 10-minute time  constraint.
-  Given that each team mate must speak/have an active role in presentation, determining when and what each teammate will present and in what order is essential.
-  Deciding whether to use visual aids to support the presentation, and if yes, what and how the team will use visuals during presentation.
-  Anticipating questions and deciding which teammate is best suited to respond.
-  Choreographing the presentation that works best in the room where team will present. ADDITIONAL POINTS FOR CONSIDERATION Given that this assignment asks you to focus on recommending a concrete change this organization  ought to make, it’s possible that the recommendations you generate for your OP report may be somewhat different. The expectation is that you will support your recommendation with evidence. That said, as with any “Preview,” it’s reasonable that you may have some gaps in the evidence. If you do, consider the following:

-  Explain the gaps carefully. Make certain you state clearly what evidence can be gathered between the Preview and when the OP report is due, as well as how you plan to gather this additional evidence.
-  Anticipate resistance from the client by having relevant, concrete evidence at hand and a clear plan for what other data may be needed.
-  Prepare for Q&A: This is where the quality of your critical thinking and your coordination as a team will be on full display. Every member of the team should be ready to answer questions.
-  If you choose to use visual aids, they must be designed and formatted to support the speaker(s) and, of course, accurate.

GRADING CRITERIA

Persuasive Strategy: Did the team have a clear, specific recommendation? Was presenting and supporting that recommendation reasonable given the time constraint? Was the argument for the recommendation framed around WIIFT (i.e., your client)? Structure: Did the structure support the persuasive strategy? Was there a clear introduction and conclusion that took advantage of the Audience Memory Curve? Did transitions help the audience follow from one point to another? Evidence: Did the team support the argument with effective, credible evidence? Q&A Management: Did team members display effective active listening, share credible responses, and reinforce the main point of the recommendation? Were questions handed off to team members as appropriate? Was a wrap-up provided at the end of Q&A? Did the team manage the Q&A within the 5-minute time constraint? Delivery: Did presenting team member(s) look and sound credible and confident? Did the delivery help the audience engage with the content? Were team members and the topic introduced effectively? Was the tone professional and consistent throughout the presentation? If applicable, were transitions between speakers handled smoothly? Did the team finish the formal remarks within the 10-minute time constraint? Visual Content If Used: Did visual aids contribute to message? Did team members focus interaction on the audience rather than the visual? Did slides follow the principles of good design (e.g., designed to support presenter; data to ink ratio maximized)?
