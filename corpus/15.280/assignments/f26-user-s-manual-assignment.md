15.280 Communication for Leaders

User’s Manual Assignment Details

“My User Manual is one of the ways I practice leading out loud. It’s a living document that describes my innate wiring and my growing edge, while putting it out to the world that I know I am – and aim to always be — a work-in-progress.”
- Abby Falik, founder and CEO of Global Citizen Year

In the spirit of working together to build a learning culture of problem solvers, listeners, writers and speakers, as part of the 15.280 Communication for Leaders course, we invite you to create a “User’s Manual” describing your goals, work style, strengths, and preferences. This gives you an opportunity for self-reflection that will help your colleagues understand who you are and how to engage with you most productively. So much is lost in virtual environments that we must all be much more deliberate about explicitly transmitting information that can be absorbed when we are in person.

Directions You are at the start of a new endeavor as an MIT Sloan MBA and have recently been placed in your cohort team. You will be working very closely with these colleagues for the next three months. Building on your interactions thus far, what would you tell your teammates about yourself that will improve how you work together? These thoughts will be the basis for your User’s Manual, a document of up to two pages that you will create and share with your Professor, TAs and teammates. We encourage you NOT to use AI for this assignment. Your User’s Manual & Comm Labs #1 and #5 In Comm Lab #1, you will share key elements of your User’s Manual with your core team, including your goals for the MBA experience and the leadership and communication skills you hope to develop. Five weeks later, in Comm Lab #5, the “Leadership Development Lab,” you will revisit those goals through a structured feedback conversation, exchanging observations, encouragement, and constructive feedback on one another’s progress. This discussion is an opportunity to reflect on your growth, gain insight from your teammates, and identify areas for continued development.

Questions to Ask Yourself (and Answer) For Your User’s Manual You may struggle to answer all the questions below in the space of your User’s Manual. That challenge is deliberate. Be succinct and give the most focus to the questions you think will be most helpful to your colleagues.

-  What are my overall goals while at MIT Sloan?
-  What near-term leadership skills do I want to develop and how can my team help me in that endeavor?
-  What do I consider to be my strengths as a communicator?
-  What communication & interpersonal skills am I working on and how could my team help me?
-  How do I prefer to communicate (e.g., style, channel, frequency, time of day)?
-  What do I value in a working relationship?
-  Which of my cultural and/or language norms are important for me to share?
-  What do I find easy and what do I find challenging when giving constructive feedback?
-  How do I prefer to receive feedback?
-  How do I tend to react to conflict?
-  What guidelines should we establish for disclosing generative AI tools in our team assignments?

-  What are my idiosyncrasies, that is, what are the individual quirks that anyone working with me should know about?
