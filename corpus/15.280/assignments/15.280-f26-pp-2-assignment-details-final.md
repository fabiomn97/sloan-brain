15.280 PERSUASIVE PRESENTATION 2

Assignment Details

This assignment includes three deliverable and constitutes 20% of your final 15.280 grade:

1) An 8-minute Persuasive *Interactive* Presentation scheduled outside of class time; 2) A Presentation Planner + Shield due at the time of your presentation; 3) A Self-Assessment of your Presentation due 48 hours after your presentation.

PERSUASIVE *INTERACTIVE* PRESENTATION

Task: Create and deliver an appropriately scoped persuasive presentation that convinces a specific audience to accept a specific recommendation or take a specific course of action. You are strongly encouraged to build off Persuasive Presentation 1. This presentation offers an opportunity to strengthen your persuasive argument. That may involve a rescoped recommendation, new evidence, more implementation details and/or more time spent addressing counterarguments. This presentation also offers an opportunity to work on the “areas for improvement” identified in your first Self-Assessment and other feedback. If you would like to work with a different topic and/or a recommendation completely unrelated to your first presentation, consult with your professor in advance of this assignment to ensure it fulfills all requirements. You must still create original content, draw upon professional interests, and choose a non-Sloan audience. Objective: You will further develop a specific and measurable communication objective (i.e. what do you want your audience TO DO) and prepare for likely opposition. Sample objectives and opposition include:

-  Persuade your company's management to pilot a company-wide community service day. Possible Opposition: Why is it worth it to take one full workday from everyone in the organization? Will there be backlash from employees who feel that we are only doing this for PR? Do we have the budget for this? And if so, is this the best use for it?
-  Persuade your company's HR team to add personal finance training to new employee onboarding. Possible Opposition: The employee training is already too long – why is it crucial we add this? Who is going to develop this training and how much work will it be to create?
-  Persuade your manager to start a weekly "lunch and learn" with other teams at the company. Possible Opposition: Why shouldn’t we use the time/budget for an offsite to improve our own team’s cohesion vs. cross-team collaboration? Do we have the budget for this? Who is going to take on the responsibility of organizing this every week (and who is going to create the agenda every week)? Timing: You have 8 minutes for this presentation. You must invite and encourage your audience to ask questions at any time during your presentation (that’s the interactive part). You need to plan your content and pace accordingly as you will be interrupted frequently with challenging questions. Prior to the start of your presentation, you will have one minute to set up your audience. This means providing those in your presentation session with key information they will need to take on their audience roles. Additional Situational Guidance: For this assignment, imagine that your audience for Persuasive Presentation 1 responded positively and asked for another meeting to focus more intently on your recommendation. You will have more time and recognize there will be some opposition to your idea. It’s likely there will be some new audience members, potentially including higher-ranking executives and/or executives who have a particular interest in your proposal and its impact. Consider how your argument and audience should evolve as your recommendation is discussed more intently. Visual aids: Visual aids, created by you for the express purpose of this assignment, are required. Date & location: Presentations are held outside of Class and Comm Lab; the sign-up process will be shared by your Professor and TA.

PRESENTATION PLANNER

The Presentation Planner + Shield Framework are designed to help you think strategically about how you build a persuasive, logical and benefit-driven argument supported by concrete evidence and deliver it effectively to your target audience. Your Professor and TA will refer to your Planner + Shield as they grade your presentation. The quality of both is factored into your overall grade for this assignment; failure to submit them will lower your assignment grade. Both are due at the time of your presentation. AUDIENCE FEEDBACK AND SELF-ASSESSMENT

You will receive audience feedback from peers who attend your presentation session with a focus on how it can be more clear, credible, and convincing. Also, you will watch the recording of your presentation, assess the strength of your persuasive argument and delivery, and next steps for improvement. To best assess your presentation, watch your recording at least twice. If you want to isolate your nonverbal delivery, watch the video only (with sound off). To isolate your verbal delivery, watch without the video (sound only). Video and sound on give you the full effect and provide the most effective way to gauge your overall persuasiveness. Your Self-Assessment should be specific, credible, and thorough, and address each grading rubric category below. For each category, you should identify at least two “strengths” and two “areas for improvement” with supporting details and specific moments from your presentation cited. No more than 1 page. The quality of your Self-Assessment is factored into your overall grade for this assignment. Failure to submit within 48 hours of your presentation will lower your assignment grade. GRADING RUBRIC

These criteria reflect the key elements that contribute to an effective persuasive presentation. The presentation will be evaluated on whether it meets, exceeds, or falls below the expected criteria as detailed below. STRATEGY:

1. There is a specific and measurable communication objective, i.e., what you want the audience TO DO because of your presentation, that is appropriately scoped for the time allotted. 2. There is one core message that is clear, concise, and memorable. 3. The presentation establishes WIIFT and is persuasive. STRUCTURE:

1. Direct or indirect structure chosen and executed based on accurate analysis of the situation and info the audience needs, plus recognition that objections and counterarguments should be addressed 2. Introduction attracts attention, sets appropriate context, and gives a preview of the presentation; conclusion reiterates core message and communicates a call-to-action for the audience. 3. Transitions between points ensure presentation is easy to follow and, as necessary, include signposting. EVIDENCE:

1. The cited evidence persuades the audience to accept your recommendation. 2. The cited evidence is accurate, credible, current, aligned with supporting points and clearly sourced.

DELIVERY:

1. Verbal delivery is dynamic and engaging with variation in tone, pace, volume, and absence of filler words. 2. Non-verbal delivery displays confidence and presence; this includes effective eye contact, open posture, purposeful movement, absence of fidgeting or other distracting behaviors. 3. Timing—you spend appropriate amount of time on critical aspects of argument and don’t exceed the 8 minutes.

Q&A MANAGEMENT:

1. Guidelines for audience participation are set at the start of the presentation. 2. Noticeable effort to respond in real time to build audience rapport e.g. active listening, paraphrasing. 3. Questions are answered credibly and reinforce main message.

VISUAL AIDS:

1. Slide headlines clearly indicate the single key takeaway of the slide. 2. Slide design is free of clutter. 3. Sources are cited on the slides; agenda slide previews the content for the audience.
