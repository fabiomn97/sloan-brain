**Points:** 10.0

15.280 Communication for Leaders is designed to be a highly interactive Class and Lab. The more prepared and invested you are in what happens in each session, the more you will learn and the more others will learn from you. Your ability to explain, defend, and modify your ideas—a critical leadership skill—will be bolstered by your advanced preparation.

Over the course of the semester, you will develop your own portfolio of participation in Class and in Lab. You can excel in this area if you complete work assigned in advance, are on time, stay through to the end of class, and prepare to contribute in the following ways:

- Provide strong evidence of having prepared in advance - including completion of readings
- Share learning from preparation and advance the discussion by contributing insightful comments
- Offer constructive feedback with your peers when appropriate
- Listen attentively and demonstrate interest in your peers’ comments, questions, and/or presentations.

Participation will be graded for each Class and Lab on the following scale:

Attends and engages with the topic by making multiple significant contributions = 9
Attends and participates with at least one comment during discussion = 8
Attends and listens actively, but does not participate verbally = 7
Excused absence = 6
Unexcused absence = 0

Students are expected to come to class and lab having read the readings and completed any additional preparation required for that day’s discussion.
