###### 15.280 Communication for Leaders
Self-Assessment of your Persuasive Presentation I

For more information, please refer to the Assignment details.

For your self-assessment, you will watch the recording of your presentation, assess the strength of your persuasive argument and delivery, and identify next steps for improvement. To best assess your presentation, watch your recording at least three times. Watch the video only (with sound off), to isolate your non-verbal delivery. Watch without the video (sound only) to isolate your verbal delivery. Watch with video and sound on give you the full effect and provide the most effective way to gauge your overall persuasiveness.

Your Self-Assessment should be specific, credible, and thorough, and address each grading rubric category in the assignment details. For each category, you should identify at least two “strengths” and two “areas for improvement” with supporting details and specific moments from your presentation cited.

Failure to submit the Self-Assessment will lower your grade on the presentation.

**Submitting Your Self-Assessment**

- **File Format**: .doc, .docx, .pdf , or via online text entry
- **Due**: Within 48 hours after your Presentation
- **Submission**: Via this Canvas Assignment or your professors preferred method
- **Length:** up to 1 page
