Communication for Leaders (15.280) is designed to help you:

-  Use a strategic communication framework for professional communication.
-  Develop and deliver persuasive arguments both in presentations and written documents.
-  Communicate effectively and interpersonally within a group. We examine a range of successful practices and guidelines derived from research and experience and give you the opportunity to develop and practice your skills as well as provide you with feedback to help you strengthen them.

The 15.280 Lab meets for one hour in 8 sessions throughout the semester.

ASSIGNMENTS – DETAILS FOR EACH ASSIGNMENT INCLUDED ON CANVAS

USER’S MANUAL

Write a User’s Manual to share with your Professor, TA and colleagues on how you like to work/communicate/interact as well as your leadership development goals. Your User’s Manual allows you to reflect critically on how you communicate, lead, give/receive feedback, as well as identify those unique skills and preferences that anyone working with you needs to know. Your User’s Manual is not graded, failure to submit will impact your participation grade. Post to Canvas on or before 9/11.

PERSUASIVE PRESENTATION I AND SELF-ASSESSMENT (20%)

Your First Persuasive Presentation + Q&A: In a session scheduled outside of class, you will create, rehearse and deliver a 4minute persuasive presentation with 2 minutes of Q&A. You will choose a business subject for a specific audience (non-MIT Sloan), persuading that audience to enact a change or pursue a course of action. Note: You may not use any visual aids (no notes, PowerPoint slides, props, etc.) for this presentation. Your Self-Assessment: Within 48 hours of the presentation, you will watch your video and assess the strength of the argument, clarity of message, management of Q&A, and effectiveness of delivery. The Self-Assessment should synthesize your observations in a narrative format, including two areas of strength and two areas of development to focus on for future presentations. Failure to submit (post to Canvas) within 48 hours of your presentation will lower your assignment grade.

PERSUASIVE PRESENTATION II AND SELF-ASSESSMENT (20%) Your Second Persuasive Presentation is also scheduled outside of class and gives you an opportunity to deliver an 8- minute *interactive* persuasive presentation using visuals/PPT and encouraged to build from your first presentation to strengthen your persuasive argument for interactive format that addresses counterarguments, and/or additional implementation details. This presentation also offers an opportunity to work on the “areas for improvement” identified in your first Self- Assessment and other feedback. Your Self-Assessment: Within 48 hours of the presentation you will watch your video and assess the strength of the argument, the clarity of message, management of interaction, and effectiveness of your delivery and visuals. Self-Assessment should synthesize your observations in a narrative format, including two areas of strength and two areas of development to focus on for future presentations. Failure to submit (post to Canvas) within 48 hours of your presentation will lower your assignment grade.

TEAM PERSUASIVE PRESENTATION WITH Q&A (10%) Based on the organization your team studied for your project in Organizational Processes (15.311), the team will create, rehearse and deliver a persuasive 15-minute presentation that includes 5 minutes of Q&A. The focus of the presentation will be one recommendation for an audience of appropriate client/leadership-level decision makers in the organization you studied. All members of the team must present, and the team will receive the same grade in the presentation.

REFLECTION REPORT (10%)

You have done considerable work to improve your leadership communication skills this semester. Please write a two- page report to your Professor and TA describing your progress as a strategic communicator as a result of this course. Include the following components and support with evidence from course readings and feedback you received.

- What you accomplished this semester that helped you to become a more mindful leader and strategic communicator.
- A story that highlights a critical communication experience you’ve had this semester (e.g., a time when you gave

or received meaning feedback or reached an audience with a persuasive argument).
- The specific and attainable communication goals for your time at MIT Sloan and how you plan to achieve them.
