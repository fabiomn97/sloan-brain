## Page 21

Understand the Communicator- Audience Relationship

Identity Trust Objective

What is your credibility with the audience?

What do you want? Is it specific/reasonable?

Who are you? Who is the audience?

How do they feel about the topic?

What is level of trust between you and audience?

What do audience members want? What are their goals/agenda?

Dear Plant Procurement Managers,

As DRW Technologies' first Corporate Vice President of Procurement, I'm implementing a new review policy to give us better visibility into major spending commitments and ensure we're leveraging our full purchasing power as a company.

Effective immediately: Any contract valued at $250,000 or more must be submitted to my office for review at least two weeks before the intended signing date. Please include the vendor, pricing, business rationale, and current draft agreement. Submit to [contact/process TBD].

I recognize this adds a step to your timelines, so please build it in going forward. If a contract is time-sensitive and can't accommodate the full two weeks, flag it to me as early as possible so we can expedite.

I'll be reaching out individually in the coming weeks to introduce myself. Questions in the meantime are welcome.

Best regards, Ed Claiborne Vice President of Procurement DRW Technologies

Subject: Introduction and Request for a Quick Virtual Conversation

Dear Plant Procurement Managers,

As many of you know, I've joined DRW Technologies as the company's first Corporate Vice President of Procurement, with a mandate to help us reduce costs across the organization. I know several of you have already begun exploring how we might leverage our collective purchasing power across plants, and I want to build on that momentum rather than duplicate it or impose something that doesn't fit how you operate.

Before putting any new process in place, I'd like to get your input directly. I'm reaching out to schedule a 30-minute virtual one-on-one with each of you over the next few weeks to understand your current contract review practices, hear your perspective on where cross-plant coordination could help (or get in the way), and talk through an idea I have for a lightweight way to flag large contracts ($250,000+) so we can spot opportunities to pool volume and negotiate better terms together.

Your autonomy in running procurement for your plant isn't changing — my goal is to add value on top of it, not slow you down. I want whatever we put in place to reflect real input from the people closest to the work.

My assistant will follow up shortly to find a time that works with your schedule. In the meantime, feel free to reach out directly with any thoughts.

Best regards,

Ed Claiborne Vice President of Procurement DRW Technologies
