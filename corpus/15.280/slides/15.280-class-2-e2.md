## Page 14

We’ve all heard non-actionable feedback statements

Sofia is unprofessional.

Blayne adds no value.

Min is not a team player.

We just can’t trust Dante.

Shankar is a poor manager.

## Page 15

Why non-actionable feedback is problematic

Sofia is unprofessional. Comments attack the person rather than person’s behavior.

Blayne adds no value. Assertions are vague or abstract.

Min is not a team player. Specific examples are missing.

We just can’t trust Dante. Range of application is too broad.

Shankar is a poor manager. Impact (& expected modifications) of behavior not clear.

## Page 34

Exercise: Giving & Receiving Feedback Mini Case

1 Feedback Giver, 1 Feedback Receiver, 1 Observer (groups of 3, not on core team)

Assign roles: Role A, Role B and Role C

Read the Case prompt for YOUR ROLE ONLY and prepare (2 minutes)

Feedback Conversation (4 minutes):  The person giving feedback starts a 4-minute conversation with the feedback recipient based on the case. During the conversation, the observer observes the speaker and listener, taking notes on observations.

Debrief (3 minutes): Observer will begin a debrief/feedback for 4 minutes. What worked well? What didn’t? What else did you notice?
