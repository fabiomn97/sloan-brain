## Page 36

Provide feedback to your partner on Shield

1. Exchange Shields 2. Read your partner’s Shield and think about the questions below silently

3. Share your feedback with each other

Use the questions below as a guide to share feedback with your partner:

1. If this audience believed all the key points, would that be enough for them to accept the recommendation. If not, what else would they need to believe?

2. Is the evidence for each point necessary and sufficient for this audience to accept the point above it. If not, what evidence would strengthen the argument?

Impromptus – 2 minutes

- Intro – recommendation, preview your points
- Use transitions / signposting
- End strong – summarize, next step
- Don’t just present your Shield – you’ve used it as a way to structure your argument – now make the argument

- Class – provide feedback afterwards on what went well and development areas
