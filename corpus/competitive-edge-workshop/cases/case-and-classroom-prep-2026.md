### COMPETITIVE EDGE: PREPARING FOR AND PARTICIPATING IN CASE CLASSES

MBA Pre-term – August 25 & 27, 2026

- Individual Preparation

- READING THE CASE

- Study the situational context

- In most cases, the situation is set out in the first few paragraphs; however, some cases may be structured differently so you may have to look for this information.

- The situational context information should tell you the company, the industry, and what type of case you’re dealing with—finance, communication, personnel problems, planning, strategy, technology management, etc.

- Skim the rest of the case

- Note headings and subheadings—what are the categories of information covered?

- Look at the central question that the case is posing—it can often be found in the last few paragraphs of the case.

- Try to answer these broad questions:

-What sort of organization is the case about?

-What problem(s) does management face?

- Focus on the exhibits

- What type of data is given?

- If the exhibits seem “common” or routine, like quarterly or annual financial reports—Is there anything unusual about them?

- Uncommon exhibits provide clues as to the significant issues of the case

- Does anything seem missing?

- Read the case from the beginning

Reading Tips

- Underline or take notes in the margins

- Refer back to the exhibits as they are presented in the case so you can understand them in context

- Distinguish fact from opinion

- Distinguish what people say from what they are doing

- Notice what isn’t being said—what’s missing?

Questions to be considered while reading

- Who are the players?

- What are the core products and services being provided?

- What is the nature of the industry?

- What decisions need to be made and by whom?

- What are the objectives of the organization and its members?

- What are the relevant resources and constraints?

- Tips

- Try to put yourself in the role of the manager in the case

- Plan to skim the case again before class. Underlining or note-taking in a different color (on the second reading) is often helpful.

- ANALYSIS

- Analyzing the assignment

- Why is this case assigned now? How does it relate to what we’ve covered so far? To what we are supposed to cover next?

- What are the assigned questions? What do they seem to emphasize?

- What are the likely unassigned questions?

- Analyzing the case: The Goal

The goal of analysis is to identify a plan of action for the protagonist (main character) and support your recommendation with information from the case.

- General notes

- The professor may specify what type of analysis they would like you to do as there is more than one way to analyze a case.

- The different types of analysis set you up to make different contributions during class.

- If no guidance is given, use the frameworks you have been recently discussing in class.

### Team Preparation

- PURPOSE of the study groups

The study group provides an opportunity for you to test your ideas and the thinking behind them, and to rehearse to present them to the class. You might think of your group as a mini-case class. Students find this a very helpful way to prepare for case discussions.

- BEFORE CLASS:

- With your team:

- Go through the case once the way it might go in class

-Opening—What’s the case about?

-Context/People/Facts

-Interpretations—relative importance of facts; relationships among people

-Recommendations & evaluation of recommendations

- Give one another feedback regarding the content and presentation of ideas

- Discuss a strategy for getting the best contributions from your group out into the discussion. The richer the group discussion, the more everyone learns.

-What’s the best way to lead-in?

-What interpretations seem most critical?

-Which recommendations are the strongest?

- Goals of the Team Preparation

- Better understand others’ thinking in order to challenge your own

- Improve the clarity, persuasiveness, and presentation of your own ideas

- Test and redefine ideas so class time can be spent on the most productive ones

- Using AI for Case Class Preparation

- Individual faculty may set expectations for using or not using AI to assist with preparation for case discussions.

- AI may be useful for summarizing key points in a case and/or confirming your answers to discussion questions.  If a case is being used for an entire class discussion, it is advised to read the case thoroughly and prepare your answers to provided discussion questions.

- If time is challenging as it will be at times during the Core and a case is being used only as a small part of the overall class discussion, AI may be helpful for summarizing key points and helping with or verifying answers to discussion questions.

### Participating in the Case Discussion

- SKILLS

- Listening

- Building on one another’s ideas

- Challenging others’ ideas

- Opening the discussion to others

- STAGES OF THE GAME

- Leading off or Leading in

- Context

- People & Facts

- Describing the problem

- What were the objectives of each of the players?

- What is or is not happening?

- What’s going wrong?

- Proposals/Recommendations

- What could have made it go differently?

- What could each of the players have done?

- What does the manager need to do now?

- Comparing/Evaluating proposals

- Synthesis

- What have you learned from this case that can be applied to other situations?

- This may not happen during the case class—If not, you will want to do afterwards on your own or in your group

- NOTES on PARTICIPATION

- Determine the norms in each particular class

- What seems to be the instructor’s style, e.g. cold calling?

- What order does the instructor seem to use?

- What are your classmates doing?

- Check in with the Teaching Assistant (TA)

a. If you have questions about your participation, feel free to check in with the TA or the instructor: they are here to help and everyone wants you to succeed.

- Realistic challenges:

- Rarely will an opportunity occur to enter the discussion when you feel 100% confident. Don’t delay: instructors aren’t looking for perfection!

- Another student will occasionally have a viewpoint or an

interpretation that will invalidate something you’ve already said. See this as an opportunity to learn rather than failure.

- When asked for something you haven’t thought about prior to the case, you can say, “I don’t know” or “I hadn’t thought about that” and then add, “but I think we can figure it out.”

### After Class

d.	  If generalizations were not drawn during the class—make notes to yourself.

e.   Consider some study group time to compare what you each took away from the

class.

f.  Reflect—How does this link with prior cases? Things you’re learning in other

classes?

g.  Self-evaluation

- Was I sufficiently prepared?

- Did I participate?

- Did others build on my ideas?

- Did I build on others’ ideas?

### Things to consider

- Remember that your goal is not just frequent participation. You want to create a profile, over time.

- Work with your study team. Encourage one another. Provide feedback.

- Members of study groups can help one another get into a discussion by either referencing someone (“Tim was explaining this in our study team…”) or by actually yielding the floor (“Our study team identified two major problems, but Mary is the one who has the expertise to explain them…”).

- Take risks. You know the consequences of not participating; what are the benefits if you do?

- Keep a sense of perspective and a sense of humor.

### Accounting

- If you are using financial sheets, read the Notes! Much of the important data you will need is only found in the Notes at the end of the financial statements.

- Notice any particular accounting policies mentioned.

- Ask yourself if their accounting practices and policies are right for the situation/business. What methods would work better?

### Communication

- Cases in Communication focus on relationships, conflict and conflict- resolution strategies, cross-cultural communication, and other related topics.

### DMD

- Most DMD cases will have you analyze or re-analyze some data.

- As in all case and problem set work, methodology is more important than getting an exact correct number, because sometimes there are several acceptable ways to arrive at an answer, giving different answers. Focus on method and reasoning.

### Economics

### As with DMD, methodology is the focus.  Make sure you understand how the numbers are derived.

### OP

- This course is about the behaviors of companies and how they operate, from the human standpoint. Focus on the nature of the company. What kind of atmosphere is there? What kind of leadership? How are changes implemented? How are changes received? How is work rewarded? How are grievances aired? What traits are peculiar to this company or situation?

### Strategy

- Strategy cases call for a diagnosis of an organization’s current conditions so that appropriate strategic actions can be recommended regarding the firm’s intent and mission.
