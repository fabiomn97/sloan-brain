## Page 13

Take Away Points

- Understanding cost types and cost structure helps you to see the true profitability of a product or client (economic versus accounting profit).

- Key pitfalls
- Ignoring opportunity costs
- Considering sunk costs
- Not distinguishing between MC and AC (optimal level

vs. shutdown)

- Cost structure is also important for strategic issues, such as competitive dynamics and entry barriers.
