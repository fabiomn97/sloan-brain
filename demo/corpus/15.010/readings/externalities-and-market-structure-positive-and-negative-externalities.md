## Page 4

Recall “Tragedy of the Commons”

- Externality: Individual or firm sees the marginal (private) effect of actions, not the overall effect on the community as whole
- Private cost/benefit ≠ Community cost/benefit
- Addressed via joint ventures (cooperation), taxes, property rights, tradable permits

Common Property Resources:

Oil Example (Lecture 15)

- Issue:  Drill more wells, pressure drops for all wells
- Unfettered development drives individual profits to zero

900 wells, 100 barrels/well, Πi = $0, 90,000 total barrels from field
- Joint venture maximizes field profits 450 wells, 550 barrels/well, Πi = $3.04 m, 247,500 total barrels from field

## Page 6

Take Away Points

- Externalities are effects of production or consumption that are not reflected in prices.

- Externalities create distortions.

- Government intervention and mergers or acquisitions are important ways to deal with externalities.
