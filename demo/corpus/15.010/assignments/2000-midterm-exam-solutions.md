Aside:  Providing the subsidy decreases dead-weight loss!  Recall that, by selling less quantity than would be sold in a competitive market, a monopolist creates DWL.  The subsidy gives the monopolist the incentive to offer more quantity than otherwise, thereby decreasing DWL.  (More value is created by the additional trades than the cost of the program to the government.)
