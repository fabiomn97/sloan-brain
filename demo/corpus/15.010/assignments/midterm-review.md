TRUE/FALSE

The snob effect makes market demand more elastic.

FALSE:  If Rolex lowers its prices, many consumers will purchase Rolex watches because of

the price effect (i.e., from the definition of the demand curve, when P goes down, Q

demanded goes up).  Because there is a snob effect, however, the value of a Rolex will

decline, and the extent to which Q demanded increases will be reduced.  Thus, the snob effect

makes market demand less elastic.
