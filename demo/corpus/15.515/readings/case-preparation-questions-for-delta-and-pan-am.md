Class preparation questions Delta and Pan Am (For class on 2 October 2003)

1)  Given the following information, compare return on equity at Delta and Pan Am (It is not necessary to use average Shareholders’ Equity in the denominator.)

Delta Pan Am Revenue 6,915,377 3,569,041 NI 306,826 (72,728) T. Assets 5,748,355 2,149,350 S.E. 2,208,823 (305,810) Cur. A. 1,651,238 850,846 Cur. L. 1,337,233 1,265,208

a) Decompose Return on Equity into Asset Turnover, Return on Sales, and

Assets/S.E. What does this decomposition say about the source of the difference between Delta’s and Pan Am’s return performance?

b)  Compare the current ratios of these companies.  What does Pan Am’s current

ratio imply?

2)  Do questions 1, 2, and 3 at the end of the case.  Question 4 involves leases, a topic we will cover later in the semester.
